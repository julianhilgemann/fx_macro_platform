# Personal Tax, Residency & Investor-Migration Parameters — 21 Jurisdictions

**Research date: 17 September 2026.** Base year 2025 as requested, with **every change already enacted or announced through 2028** dated. Germany is the baseline reference.

**Sourcing rule applied:** primary sources (tax authorities, ministries, official gazettes, HMRC/IRS/NTA/IRD/Immigration NZ/Agenzia delle Entrate) where obtainable, otherwise Big-4 or law-firm guides. Anything not pinned to a primary or authoritative secondary source is marked **⚠** and listed in **§5 Confidence**. Nothing is guessed.

**Not verified — do not rely on these.**

1. **Switzerland — largely closed.** Now verified: residents are taxed on **worldwide income and worldwide net wealth**; federal top marginal **11.5%**, Zurich top basic **13%**, Geneva **18.00%**, giving **combined ≈26% (ZH) / ≈38% (GE)**; **cantonal wealth tax, federal exempt, top rates 0.1%–0.87%** with worked examples (CHF 1m couple: **CHF 1,253** Stans vs **CHF 6,804** Neuchâtel; exemptions ZH CHF 161,000 / SZ CHF 250,000 / NW none); **dividend partial taxation = 70% inclusion federal, ≥50% cantonal** (≥10% shareholding); **cantonal inheritance tax for non-descendants** (Geneva to 26%, Vaud 22–50%, Zurich 12–36%; Schwyz/Obwalden nil; tax due in the canton of the deceased's last domicile); **Eigenmietwert abolished by 2025 referendum, in force planned 2029**. **Still unverified:** **AHV/ALV/BVG contribution rates and ceilings**; **Circular 36 / transSAFE safe-harbour thresholds** for professional securities dealing; exact cantonal inheritance rates **in every canton**; and whether **Nidwalden** has fully abolished inheritance tax (Polia says yes, Homburger omits it).
2. **Ireland — primary-confirmed, including its exit-tax position.** The remittance basis (s.71(2)–(3) TCA 1997), the €200,000 domicile levy and the **8-year deemed disposal** are all confirmed live from Finance Act 2024/2025, Revenue TDM Part 05-01-21A (reviewed May 2026) and Revenue's SARP conditions page (published 22 Jan 2026). Residual ⚠: the exact **USC band structure** and the precise **combined 52% marginal** arithmetic.
   **Portugal — upgraded.** AIMI (CIMI arts. 135.º-A–L), the CIRS art. 43.º n.º 5 holding-period relief and the CIRS art. 10.º-A exit-tax rule are all primary-sourced; residual ⚠: whether AT in practice grants art. 43.º n.º 5 relief on **foreign**-listed securities (Abreu Advogados reports AT restricted it to Annex G disposals in the 2025 filing season — a live dispute, argued contrary to statute and Art. 63 TFEU), the date of Portaria 52-A/2025/1 (AT documents conflict: 25 Jan vs 25 Feb 2025), and the AIMI low-income exemption (~€153,300).
3. **Netherlands — improved but still partial.** Now verified: **there is no CGT** (capital income is taxed only via the Box 3 deemed return — 36%; deposits 1.28%, investments 6.00%, debts 2.70%; heffingsvrij vermogen €59,357 / €118,714); **inheritance top rate 40%**; **Box 2 is 24.5%/31% in both 2025 and 2026** (33% was 2024 only; bracket-1 €68,843 from 1 Jan 2026); and a **Box 2 exit tax on ≥5% holdings EXISTS via art. 4.16(1)(h) + art. 2.8(2) + art. 9.1 Wet IB** (art. 7.5 is the *non-resident* AB provision, **not** the exit tax). **Still unverified:** **Box 1 2026 brackets** and the €75–80k effective marginal; *zelfstandigenaftrek* / *MKB-winstvrijstelling*; the **box-2 exit-tax rate, deferral and EU-law status**; *erfbelasting* non-descendant thresholds; whether the **27% ruling date survives** the parliamentary process.
4. **Spain.** Employee **social-security rate and maximum contribution base**; the **50% dividend exemption** (art. 21 LIRPF) and the derived 32–36% combined figure; **inheritance tax for non-descendants** (34% state scale, €100,000 group III/IV reduction, multiplier coefficients, regional rebates); **art. 95 bis exit-tax mechanics** and the **scope of the CJEU referral**.
5. **UAE.** The **AED 1,000,000** natural-person business turnover threshold and personal-investment carve-out; GPSSA rates; corporate deemed disposal; golden-visa thresholds; DMTT start date. ATO/IRAS/tax.gov.ae/MAS/CPF and BOE are either WAF-403 or undecodable PDFs.
6. **Singapore.** Top rate **24%** and YA2025 brackets; CPF 20%/17%; the no-CGT and one-tier-dividend authorities; estate-duty abolition date; ABSD 60%; **s.10L/FSIE scope**; **GIP thresholds**; IIR/DMTT status.
7. **Poland — exit tax verified; two new items and one unresolved conflict.** Confirmed: art. 30da PIT (**PLN 4m, 19%/3%, collection deferred**); **Poland DOES have a 5% IP Box for individuals** (I had wrongly said "none identified"); *podatek liniowy* 19% and *ryczałt* 2–17%; health-contribution floor PLN 498.35/month from 1 Jan 2026. **Poland DOES levy *podatek od spadków i darowizn* — PwC's "NP" entry for Poland is unreliable.** **Group 0** (spouse, descendants, ascendants, siblings, stepchildren, parents-in-law) has been **exempt since 1 Jan 2007**; **Group III (unrelated) rates remain ⚠ unverified**. **⚠ UNRESOLVED CONFLICT: two research streams read the same sources and reached opposite conclusions on whether a lump-sum new-resident regime exists** (one reports **PLN 200,000/yr for up to 10 years**, +PLN 100,000/yr per family member, requiring no Polish residence in ≥5 of the prior 6 years; the other found **no such regime**). Verify before relying on either. Also ⚠: employee ZUS rates/ceiling; the dividend participation exemption; whether the end-2027 deferral regulation was signed; the status of **III SA/Wa 566/25**.
8. **Czechia — largely closed.** Now verified: **inheritance is exempt for ALL beneficiaries** (ZDP §4a(1)(a), no relationship test; relationship matters only for **gifts**, where nephews/nieces, siblings, uncles/aunts and the spouse's relatives are exempt, and other donors get CZK 50,000/donor/year); employee social **11.6%**; **23% thresholds CZK 1,676,052 (2025) / 1,762,812 (2026)**; max social base 2,234,736 / 2,350,416 with **no health ceiling**; the **40m securities test repealed from 1 Jan 2026 by Act 360/2025** (retained for crypto); **no personal exit tax** (§23g is corporate-only); **no IP box at any level**; flat-tax bands 8,716/16,745/27,139 (2025) and 9,984/16,745/27,139 (2026); self-employed monthly floors CZK 7,902 (2025) and ~CZK 8,311–9,026 (2026). Residual ⚠: **2027 figures are announced, not enacted**; crypto CGT interaction detail.
