## 2. SPAIN

- **Rule + citation.** Art. **95 bis** Ley 35/2006 (LIRPF), heading *"Ganancias patrimoniales por cambio de residencia"*. Added by art. 1.61 of Ley 26/2014, published 28/11/2014, in force 01/01/2015. The BOE consolidated text (last updated **09/09/2026**) records **no later amendment** to art. 95 bis. → [BOE consolidated Ley 35/2006, art. 95 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a95bis)
- **Trigger — residence test. Exact statutory wording in force:**
  > *"…siempre que el contribuyente hubiera tenido tal condición durante al menos **diez de los quince períodos impositivos** anteriores al último período impositivo que deba declararse por este impuesto…"*
  → **10 of the previous 15 tax periods.** There is **no "5 of 10" test** in art. 95 bis. Confirmed in a second primary text: [Ministerio de Hacienda consolidated LIRPF art. 95 bis](https://www.hacienda.gob.es/DocLeyes/onlinelt/C02.01.2006.11.28.ley.35.2006.irpf_14237.htm). **The "5" in the brief is a misattribution** — the five-year figure in art. 95 bis is the *aplazamiento* return window (apartado 4).
- **Trigger — thresholds (unchanged 2025 and 2026).** Either limb suffices:
  - (a) combined market value of the shares/participations **exceeds €4,000,000**; **or**
  - (b) if (a) is not met — participation in the entity **exceeds 25%** at the accrual date of the last period to be declared **and** the market value of that entity's shares **exceeds €1,000,000**; in that case only the gains on those shares are caught.
  → BOE text above; corroborated [Forvis Mazars, *Exit tax due to change of tax residence in Spanish PIT*](https://www.forvismazars.com/es/en/insights/articulos/exit-tax-due-to-change-of-tax-residence-in-spanish) and [Forvis Mazars (ES)](https://www.forvismazars.com/es/es/insights/articulos/cambio-de-residencia-fiscal-en-el-irpf-espanol).
- **"25%" is a threshold, not a rate.** Art. 95 bis contains **no 25% tax rate**. **UNVERIFIED as a rate.**
- **Valuation rules** (art. 95 bis.3): listed securities at quotation; unlisted at the higher of net equity per the last closed balance sheet or capitalisation at 20% of the 3-year average result; collective investment institutions at liquidating value.
- **Rate + base (2025 and 2026).** The gain forms part of the **renta del ahorro** (art. 46.b) and is imputed to the last period to be declared. The savings scale is the sum of the state scale (art. 66.1) and the autonomic scale (art. 76), both currently **9.5% / 10.5% / 11.5% / 13.5% / 15%** → combined **19% / 21% / 23% / 27% / 30%** (top rate above €300,000). Base = market value − acquisition value. → [art. 66](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a66) · [art. 76](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a76)
- **2025 change (rate environment, not the exit-tax thresholds).** **Ley 7/2024, de 20 de diciembre** (BOE-A-2024-26694), **disposición final 7.1 y 7.2**, **with effect from 1 January 2025**, modified arts. 66.1 and 76: *"Con efectos desde el 1 de enero de 2025, se establece en el **30%** el tipo de gravamen que aplica cuando la base liquidable del ahorro sea superior a 300.000 euros, siendo la suma de la escala general y escala autonómica."* → [Inverco note on Ley 7/2024](https://www.inverco.es/archivosdb/ap.1-ley-72024-impuesto-complementario-y-modificacin-del-tipo-de-gravamen-de-la-base-liquidable-del-ahorro.pdf) · BOE amendment notes at the art. 66 / art. 76 links above. **The top rate before 1 Jan 2025: UNVERIFIED** (not confirmed from a primary source).
- **Deferral / payment options.**
  - **Art. 95 bis.4 — *aplazamiento* on request.** Available where the change of residence results from a temporary posting for work reasons to a non-tax-haven country, or for any other reason where the temporary move is to a country with a DTT containing an information-exchange clause. The LGT (Ley 58/2003) applies, **including accrual of interest and the constitution of guarantees**; guarantees may be constituted wholly or partly **over the very shares** concerned. The deferral expires at the latest on **30 June of the year following the end of the five-year period**. If the taxpayer reacquires contributor status **within the 5 tax years** following the last period to be declared without having transferred the shares, **the deferred debt and the accrued interest are extinguished**. For work postings an extension may be requested, capped at **5 additional years**.
  - **Art. 95 bis.5** — on reacquiring contributor status without having transferred the shares, the taxpayer may request rectification of the self-assessment and obtain a refund of the amounts paid (late-payment interest runs from the payment date).
  - **Art. 95 bis.6 — EU/EEA option ("sursis"-equivalent).** For a move to an **EU member state or an EEA state with effective tax information exchange**, the taxpayer may opt for the gain to be **self-assessed only if, within the 10 tax years following the last period to be declared**: (1) the shares are transferred *inter vivos*; (2) the taxpayer ceases to be resident in an EU/EEA state; or (3) the notification obligation in letter (c) is breached. Under (1) the gain is reduced by the positive difference between the departure-date market value and the actual transfer value, that transfer value being increased by distributions or other perceptions that reduced the entity's net worth after the loss of contributor status (unless already taxed under IRNR). The taxpayer must notify the administration of the option, the gain, the new state of residence and address, later changes, and continued ownership. If the taxpayer reacquires contributor status without any triggering event, art. 95 bis ceases to apply (letter d).
  - **Art. 95 bis.7** — specialities for moves to a tax-haven jurisdiction where residence is not lost under art. 8.2.
  - **Art. 95 bis.8** — for taxpayers who opted for the *régimen de trabajadores desplazados* (Beckham regime), the 10-period clock starts from the first period in which the special regime no longer applies.
- **2026 changes: none identified** for art. 95 bis (BOE consolidated text current to 09/09/2026 shows no post-2015 amendment).
- **Also note:** art. 95 bis covers only **shares/participations** (and collective investment institutions) — not other asset classes.

## 3. NETHERLANDS

- **Citation correction (important).** **Art. 7.5 Wet IB 2001 is *not* the exit-tax provision** — it governs the taxable income of **non-residents** from a Dutch *aanmerkelijk belang* (and, in lid 6–8, two points that touch exit tax: a company ≥5 years in NL stays deemed NL-resident while a conserverende aanslag on an AB in it is unpaid; moving a company's place of effective management out of NL is a disposal of the AB). → [art. 7.5 Wet IB 2001](https://wetten-overheid.nl/wet/BWBR0011353/artikel/7.5) · [wetten.overheid.nl, Wet IB 2001 in force 01-01-2026](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-01-01)
- **Correct chain of authority.**
  - **Art. 4.16(1)(h) Wet IB 2001** — *"het anders dan door overlijden ophouden binnenlands belastingplichtige te zijn, hieronder begrepen de situatie waarin de belastingplichtige voor de toepassing van … een verdrag ter voorkoming van dubbele belasting wordt geacht geen inwoner van Nederland meer te zijn"* counts as **vervreemding** (fictieve vervreemding). → [art. 4.16](https://wetten-overheid.nl/wet/BWBR0011353/artikel/4.16)
  - **Art. 2.8(2)** designates the gain as **te conserveren inkomen**; **art. 9.1** provides for levy by **conserverende aanslag**. → [Belastingdienst, Kennisgroepstandpunt KG:041:2026:2 (23-03-2026)](https://kennisgroepen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/kg04120262-conserverende-aanslag-box-2-minderjarig-kind/) · [FISIN 2026, ch. 26 *Te conserveren inkomen*](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/te_conserveren_inkomen)
- **Trigger / threshold.** AB status under **art. 4.6 Wet IB 2001**: **at least 5%** of the placed capital, or rights to acquire ≥5%, or profit-sharing certificates ≥5%, or ≥5% of votes in a co-operative — alone or together with the fiscal partner; family attribution can also create an AB. → [art. 4.6](https://wetten-overheid.nl/wet/BWBR0011353/artikel/4.6) · [Belastingdienst Box 2](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/box_2)
- **No 10-year shareholding look-back located.** Nothing in arts. 4.6 or 4.16 imposes a look-back period for AB status. **The "10 years" in the Dutch system is (i) the validity period of a *standard* conserverende aanslag — which explicitly does not apply to AB — and (ii) the pre-15-September-2015 AB emigration regime** (see Deferral). **UNVERIFIED as an AB look-back rule.** → Belastingdienst Buitenland, [*Conserverende aanslag bij emigratie*](https://www.id12.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/buitenland/content/conserverende-aanslag-bij-emigratie) · FISIN 2026 ch. 26 §26.1: *"Na 10 jaar kunt u kwijtschelding … Dit geldt niet als het gaat om een conserverende aanslag in verband met aanmerkelijk belang."*
- **Rate — box 2 (the answer to the 24.5% / 27% question).** From the Belastingdienst's own rate table:
  | Year | Bracket 1 | Rate 1 | Bracket 2 | Rate 2 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 2024 | to €67,000 | 24.5% | from €67,000 | **33%** |
  | 2025 | to €67,804 | 24.5% | from €67,804 | **31%** |
  | 2026 | to €68,843 | 24.5% | from €68,843 | **31%** |
  → [Belastingdienst, *Box 2: uitleg en tarieven*](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/box_2); the same 2026 figures appear in the Belastingdienst's own [FISIN 2026 ch. 14 *Aanmerkelijk belang*](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang). **So the brief's "27% in 2026" is not correct: it is 31%, and 31% already applied in 2025** (the 33% top rate was 2024 only).
- **Tax base.** Deemed disposal: transfer price and acquisition price are both taken at *waarde in het economisch verkeer* at the emigration date; the disposal gain is *overdrachtsprijs − verkrijgingsprijs*. → FISIN 2026 ch. 14 §14.5 *Vervreemdingsvoordelen* (link above).
- **Deferral.**
  - **Automatic *uitstel van betaling*** on emigration to an **EU or EEA country**; **no *invorderingsrente*** is charged over the deferral period; where the aanslag covers more than one income component, each part gets separate deferral. → Belastingdienst, *Conserverende aanslag bij emigratie* (link above).
  - **Non-EU moves:** no automatic deferral — must be requested; the Belastingdienst may require **zekerheid** (bank guarantee, mortgage right, pledge). **Exception: emigrating to Norway, Iceland or Liechtenstein — no security required** (stated both for the AB and for the *kapitaalverzekering eigen woning*). Moving on from an EU state to a non-EU state ends the automatic deferral; a fresh request is needed.
  - **AB-specific term:** emigrated **before 15 September 2015, 15:15** → **10 years** deferral, after which *kwijtschelding* may be requested on showing the business still exists and the shares are still held. Deregistered **after 15 September 2015, 15:15** → **levenslang uitstel** (deferral without time limit); the aanslag is **no longer remitted after 10 years**, so the Dutch AB gain must eventually be settled. → Belastingdienst, *Conserverende aanslag bij emigratie* (link above).
  - **Deferral ends (wholly or partly) on:** sale of the shares, distribution of dividend, cessation of the business, breach of the conditions (e.g. cashing in a pension/lijfrente). Partial breach ends deferral only for that part.
  - **Interest:** **no *invorderingsrente*** during the deferral period for EU/EEA moves (link above). **The rate applied if deferral lapses and the tax falls due: UNVERIFIED.**
- **Does the 2025+ Box 3 reform affect the AB exit tax?** **No effect identified.** The AB exit tax is a **box 2** levy (arts. 2.8, 2.12, 4.16, 9.1); the *Wet werkelijk rendement box 3* reform is confined to **box 3** (sparen en beleggen, ch. 5), and the Belastingdienst maintains separate FISIN chapters for the two ([ch. 13 box 3](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/bezittingen_en_schulden_box_3) · [ch. 14 box 2](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/aanmerkelijk_belang)). **An explicit authority stating "no interaction" was not located — the conclusion rests on the statutory structure.**
- **Key 2025 / 2026 changes.**
  - **1 Jan 2025:** box 2 second-bracket rate **33% → 31%**; bracket-1 threshold indexed **€67,000 → €67,804** (Belastingdienst table above).
  - **1 Jan 2026:** bracket-1 threshold indexed **€67,804 → €68,843**; rate structure unchanged at 24.5% / 31% (Belastingdienst table above).
  - **No change to the exit tax / conserverende aanslag mechanism** for the AB was identified in the Belastingplan 2025 or Belastingplan 2026 documents reviewed. Belastingplan 2027 was published on Prinsjesdag **15 September 2026**, with measures generally applying from 1 January 2027 — no exit-tax measure identified. → [Rijksoverheid, Belastingplan pages](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/belastingplan)
  - **Lead, not verified:** a 2025 Hoge Raad / Parket conclusion, **ECLI:NL:PHR:2025:1016**, on the *aanmerkelijkbelangbepaling* in Dutch tax treaties in relation to an emigration assessment — **content not retrieved, treat as UNVERIFIED.** → [uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl PDF endpoint](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/api/pdfdownload/ECLI_NL_PHR_2025_1016)

## 4. NORWAY

- **Rule + citation.** **Skatteloven § 10-70** — *"Skatteplikt for gevinst på aksjer og andeler mv. ved utflytting og ved overføring"* (individuals); **§ 10-71** for companies; deferral detail in **skattelovforskriften § 10-70-1** and **FSFIN § 10-70-4**. Current text enacted by **Lov 20. desember 2024 nr. 86** (Prop. 1 LS (2024–2025), Innst. 4 L (2024–2025), Lovvedtak 17 (2024–2025)). → [Lovtidend 20.12.2024 nr. 86 — full amending act (PDF)](http://eksamen.lovdata.no/static/lovtidend/ltavd1/2024/nl-20241220-086.pdf) · [Lovdata LTI record](https://lovdata.no/LTI/lov/2024-12-20-86) · [§ 10-70 on Lovdata](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/§10-70)
- **Trigger.**
  - § 10-70(1): gain on an asset listed in (2) held by an **individual taxpayer at the moment the tax liability to the realm ceases** under § 2-1(3), or when the taxpayer is treated as resident in another state under a tax treaty, **is taxable as if the asset had been realised on the last day before that moment**. The same applies on transfer of such an asset to a person, company or body not taxable under §§ 2-1/2-2 or treaty-resident elsewhere.
  - § 10-70(2) — assets in scope: (a) shares, participations and equity certificates in Norwegian companies per § 2-2(1)(a)–(e); (b) **aksjesparekonto** (§ 10-21); (c) an interest in a Norwegian company with *deltakerfastsettelse* (§ 2-2(2)); (d) shares/participations in corresponding **foreign** companies; (e) subscription rights, options and other financial instruments whose underlying object is (a)–(d) or (f); (f) capital insurance covered by § 10-20(10).
  - § 10-70(3): losses are deductible on exit/transfer to another EEA state to the same extent as gains are taxable; the deduction right lapses if the taxpayer or transferee moves outside the EEA within the deadline in (7).
  - § 10-70(6): acquisition value for assets held on immigration = market value at the time of becoming resident; reduced by gains accrued during an earlier period of Norwegian residence that were not taxed in Norway.
- **Threshold — the NOK 500,000 question.**
  - **In force from 20 March 2024: § 10-70(4).** *"Skatteplikt ved utflytting etter første ledd første punktum er begrenset til den delen av skattyters samlede gevinst som, fratrukket fradragsberettigede tap, **overstiger 3 millioner kroner**."* → a **NOK 3,000,000 basic allowance (bunnfradrag)**; only the excess is taxed. For **transfers (overføring)** under (1) second sentence the threshold is net gain/loss **exceeding NOK 100,000** in the income year. Where exit and transfer occur in the same year, the total gain on transferred assets not already taxed is included at exit. → Lovtidend PDF above.
  - **The NOK 500,000 figure was the *consultation-paper* proposal, not the enacted rule.** Finansminister Stoltenberg, written answer Dok. 15:1899 (2024–2025), answered **24 April 2025**: *"Endringen fra en beløpsgrense på **500 000 kroner i høringsforslaget** til innføring av **bunnfradrag på 3 mill. kroner i Prop. 1 LS (2024–2025)** har heller ikke påvirket provenyanslaget."* → [Stortinget, skriftlig spørsmål fra Tina Bru, qnid=104342](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Sporsmal/Skriftlige-sporsmal-og-svar/Skriftlig-sporsmal/?qnid=104342) · [same answer in the document series (PDF)](https://www.stortinget.no/globalassets/pdf/dokumentserien/2024-2025/dokument-15-13-202425.pdf)
  - **Sources disagree:** **BDO** (23 May 2025) writes that *"the existing NOK 500,000 threshold is replaced by a 3 MNOK basic allowance"*, implying NOK 500,000 was in force. The government's own answer indicates it was only a consultation proposal. **Both figures and both URLs:** BDO — [*Norway: National Budget 2025 Makes Amendments to Exit Tax Rules*](https://www.global-acc.bdo.global/en-gb/insights/tax/expatriate-tax/norway-national-budget-2025-makes-amendments-to-exit-tax-rules); government — Stortinget link above. **A primary source showing NOK 500,000 actually in force in 2024 was not located → UNVERIFIED.**
- **Rate + base.**
  - Base: market value at the deemed realisation date minus acquisition value; where no known market value exists it is set by discretion; for an aksjesparekonto the gain is deposits vs. market value of the account (§ 10-70(5)).
  - Rate: the gain is *alminnelig inntekt* taxed at **22%**, with the shareholder-model uplift factor **1.72** applied to share income → **effective 37.84%**.
  - **2026 (verified):** Skatteetaten — *"Skatt på gevinst og utbytte for inntektsåret 2026 er **37,84 prosent**, mens skatt på andre kapitalinntekter er 22 prosent"*; uplift factor **1,72**. → [Skatteetaten, *Gevinst, tap eller utbytte på aksjer*](https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/faktor-for-oppjustering-av-gevinsttap-eller-utbytte-pa-aksjer/)
  - **2025:** **37.84%** per BDO — *"Tax was levied at a **37.84% rate**, calculated on the unrealised capital gains in shares and securities as if the shares/ownership interests were realised on the day before emigration"* (BDO link above). BAHR (24 June 2026) confirms *"Skattesatsen på aksjeinntekter (37,84 %) videreføres"*. **The Skatteetaten year-selector value for 2025 was not separately retrieved → the 2025 rate rests on BDO + BAHR.**
- **Deferral / payment options (full detail).**
  - **§ 10-70(7).** The taxpayer **may be granted 12 years' deferral** of payment of the assessed tax, counted **from the end of the year in which the exit tax is timed** (§ 14-25). Condition: **betryggende sikkerhet** (satisfactory security) for the tax and any interest. **On moving or transferring to another EEA state, security is required only where there is a *reell risiko* that the tax and interest claim cannot be recovered.** Two payment methods: **(a) one twelfth per year, interest-free** (future instalments reduced proportionally on partial settlement); or **(b) payment at the end of the deferral period, with interest** from the date the tax should have been paid had deferral not been granted.
  - **Skattedirektoratet guideline**, decided **31 March 2025**, published **8 April 2025**: 12 years; choice between the interest-free 12-instalment route and payment at the end with interest; security in principle required in all cases, **but for EEA moves only where there is a real risk**; where the taxpayer offers security **in the very assets subject to exit tax, the tax office must regard it as satisfactory without further valuation** (though it must ensure the agreement is perfected); the existence of a **functioning recovery agreement** with the EEA state and the taxpayer's **payment history** are the key factors; where no functioning recovery agreement exists, real risk is always present (Prop. 1 LS (2024–2025) point 5.5.4). → [Skatteetaten, *Retningslinje for utsettelse av innbetaling av fastsatt utflyttingsskatt etter skatteloven § 10-70*](https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/rettskilder/type/retningslinjer/retningslinje-for-utsettelse-av-innbetaling-av-fastsatt-utflyttingsskatt-etter-skatteloven--10-70/)
  - **§ 10-70(8).** The deferral right lapses on **realisation, the taxpayer's death, transfer to a person etc. as in (1) second sentence, and cessation of business** in a company as in (2)(c). It is **not** lost on death if personal heirs assume the deceased's obligations. Withdrawals from an aksjesparekonto beyond repayment of deposits cause proportionate lapse. **On distribution of dividend etc. on other exit-taxed assets, the deferral lapses for an amount equal to the distribution × 0.7** — i.e. 70% of any distribution is applied to the exit-tax claim; regulation may limit this so that tax on the distribution plus the amount for which deferral lapses does not exceed 100% of the distribution (**FSFIN § 10-70-4 → up to 100%**). → Lovtidend PDF · [Wiersholm letter to ESA, 1 Sept 2025, p. 2](https://wiersholm.no/content/uploads/2025/09/2025-09-01-Brev-til-ESA.pdf)
  - **§ 10-70(9).** If the taxpayer again becomes resident in Norway (or treaty-resident in Norway) **within 12 years** after the end of the year to which the exit tax is timed, the § 10-70 taxation **falls away for the assets held at the time of return, as do accrued interest**. If return occurs **later than 12 years** and the exit tax has by then been settled in full, the original acquisition value of assets still held is adjusted by the value change subject to exit taxation; aksjesparekonto deposits adjusted correspondingly.
  - **§ 10-70(10):** for transfers by inheritance as in (8) second sentence, skatteloven § 9-7 applies correspondingly. **§ 10-70(11):** the taxpayer must give the tax authorities all information relevant to rights and obligations **unprompted and without undue delay** (heirs who have taken over assets likewise); **the deferral right ceases on breach of this duty**.
  - **§ 2-35(2)(b):** **Svalbard is equated with an EEA state** for the application of § 10-70.

## 5. FRANCE

- **Rule + citation.** **Article 167 bis CGI** — the *exit tax*, imposing latent gains on *droits sociaux, valeurs, titres ou droits* on transfer of the tax domicile outside France. Introduced by **art. 48 of the first *loi de finances rectificative* for 2011 (loi n° 2011-900 du 29 juillet 2011)**. → [Sénat, rapport général PLF 2024, l23-128-21, pp. 84–85 (PDF)](https://www.senat.fr/rap/l23-128-21/l23-128-211.pdf) · [impots.gouv.fr, *Do I have to pay an "Exit Tax"?* (page modified 10/03/2026)](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/international-particulier/questions/i-am-leaving-france-do-i-have-pay-exit-tax)
- **Trigger.**
  - **Residence test — 6 of the previous 10 years.** impots.gouv.fr: *"This affects your situation if you were a resident of France for tax purposes for at least **six years out of the previous ten** prior to the transfer of your residence abroad"* (link above). The Sénat report states the same: *"sous la condition, notamment, d'une domiciliation du contribuable en France pendant **six ans sur les dix années** précédant le transfert"* (Sénat PDF above). **The brief's "2-of-10" is not the residence test — the "2 years" is the dégrèvement period (below).**
  - **Thresholds — either limb.** Sénat report: *"Sont imposables les plus-values latentes détenues directement ou indirectement par les membres du foyer fiscal, et représentant à la date du départ **50 % des bénéfices sociaux d'une société** ou qui ont une **valeur cumulée excédant 800 000 euros**."* impots.gouv.fr states the same in English: *"you own stocks or shares whose value is **equal to or greater than €800,000** or **represents at least 50% of a company's corporate profits**."* → Sénat PDF · impots.gouv.fr (links above).
  - **Base also includes:** latent gains on the securities/rights, **receivables arising from an earn-out clause** (*clause de complément de prix*), and **gains previously placed in *report d'imposition*** (deferred taxation) — per impots.gouv.fr and the Sénat report.
- **Rate + base.**
  - Base: the difference between the value of the securities at the date of transfer and their acquisition value (reduced where applicable by the holding-period allowance under art. 150-0 D CGI or the retiring-business-manager allowance).
  - **2025: 12.8% (PFU income-tax component) + 17.2% (prélèvements sociaux) = 30.0%.**
  - **2026: 12.8% + 18.6% = 31.4%.** The PFU was *"Anciennement fixé à 30 %, … désormais fixé à **31,4 %**"*, composed *"depuis cette date : **12,8 % d'impôt sur le revenu ; 18,6 % de prélèvements sociaux**. Au 1er janvier 2026, le taux de PFU s'élève donc à 31,4 %"*, because **the CSG rate rose by 1.4 points on 1 January 2026**. → [service-public / lecomarquage, *Évolution du taux du Prélèvement Forfaitaire Unique (PFU)* (modified 10/02/2026)](https://lecomarquage.service-public.fr/actu/3.4/pro/xml/actualites/A18796.xml) · [Code de la sécurité sociale art. L136-8, version cited by that notice](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051287395) · [GPA, *[France] 2026 Finance Law raises flat tax and impacts expats and cross-border commuters*, 27 Feb 2026](https://gpa.net/blogs/emea-1/france-2026-finance-law-raises-flat-tax-and-impacts-expats-and-cross-border-commuters) — the higher rate applies to *"dividends, bond coupons, **capital gains on securities** and cryptocurrency disposals"* · [DLA Piper, *Loi de financement de la sécurité sociale 2026 : hausse de la CSG*, 7 Jan 2026](https://www.dlapiper.com/fr-fr/insights/publications/2026/01/loi-de-finance-de-la-securite-sociale-2026) · [bensaid-avocats, *Exit tax 2026*](https://www.bensaid-avocats.fr/exit-tax-2026/) — *"le taux global atteint désormais 31,4 % (CSG portée à 10,6 %)"*.
  - **Option for the progressive scale (*barème*)** remains available (service-public notice).
  - **CDHR — *contribution différentielle sur les hauts revenus*.** Created by **art. 10 of the Loi de finances 2025**; it *"assure une imposition minimale de **20 %** des plus hauts revenus"* and applies to **income received from 1 January 2025**. Taxpayers: French tax resident in 2025, **RFR > €250,000** (single/widowed/separated/divorced) or **> €500,000** (joint), and **average tax rate below 20%**; a *décote* applies where RFR is below **€330,000** (single) / **€660,000** (couple). A **95% advance** was payable between **1 and 15 December 2025**, reconciled on the 2026 return. → [impots.gouv.fr, *Contribution Différentielle Sur les Hauts Revenus (CDHR) — FAQ* (PDF)](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/media/1_metier/1_particulier/EV/1_declarer/111_cdhr/faq_cdhr.pdf) · [BOFiP BOI-IR-CDHR, 30/06/2026](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/15093-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-IR-CDHR-20260630) · [service-public, *La contribution différentielle sur les hauts revenus est maintenue*](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/actualites/A18878)
    - **Whether exit-tax latent gains are inside the CDHR base: UNVERIFIED.** No source located stating expressly whether the art. 167 bis gain enters the "*RFR retraité*" used for the CDHR computation. Do not assume either way.
- **Deferral — *sursis de paiement*.**
  - **Automatic (*de plein droit*)** where the taxpayer transfers residence to an **EU member state or an EEA state** (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein) that has an administrative-assistance convention with France, or a mutual-assistance agreement for the recovery of tax debts.
  - **On request** (form **2074-ET** + a guarantee proposal) for other destinations. Since **2019** this option is confined to transfers to a **non-cooperative state or territory**, or to a **non-EU state/territory that has not concluded** with France either a tax treaty containing an administrative-assistance clause to combat tax evasion and avoidance **or** a mutual-assistance agreement for tax recovery (art. 112 of the 2019 Budget Act, loi n° 2018-1317 du 28 décembre 2018). For transfers from **22 November 2019**, the request must be filed **at least 90 days before** the transfer (previously 30 days) (annexe III, art. 41 *tervicies* A CGI). Where deferral is not requested or is refused (e.g. insufficient guarantees), the tax is collected under ordinary rules. → impots.gouv.fr (link above) · [BOFiP BOI-RPPM-PVBMI-50-10-50 (PDF export)](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/export/pdf/16250)
  - **Conditional relief (*dégrèvement*)** for transfers from **1 January 2019**: the period after which the taxpayer may obtain relief, subject to the sursis and provided there is no sale/redemption/cancellation of the shares or liquidation of the company in the interim, was **reduced from 15 years** to:
    - **2 years** where the securities within the exit-tax scope are worth **less than €2,570,000**;
    - **5 years** where worth **more than €2,570,000**. → impots.gouv.fr (link above).
  - **On-going obligations:** while benefiting from the sursis the taxpayer must file the annual income tax return **2042**, annex **2042-C** and form **2074-ET** each year, stating the amount of tax under sursis; failure to file, or omission of information, makes the deferred tax due if not regularised within 30 days of a formal notice. On expiry of the sursis the taxpayer files the same forms the following year and pays the tax; **a 10% increase under art. 1730 CGI** applies to tax not paid by that deadline. On death, the heirs file and pay the following year. → BOFiP BOI-RPPM-PVBMI-50-10-50 (link above).
  - **Guarantees** are required for the on-request sursis; they are lifted if the taxpayer subsequently transfers residence to an EU/EEA state (the *sursis de droit* is substituted), and the fiscal-representation obligation ends at the same date. → BOFiP (link above).
  - **Interest on the deferred amount: UNVERIFIED.** The BOFiP material retrieved specifies the **10% increase under art. 1730 CGI** for late payment at the end of the sursis but does not describe a running *intérêt de retard* accruing during the sursis period. Do not assert one.
- **Key 2025 / 2026 changes.**
  - **Loi de finances pour 2025 (loi n° 2025-127 du 14 février 2025)** — created the **CDHR** (art. 10), effective for income **from 1 January 2025** (impots.gouv.fr CDHR FAQ, link above). **No amendment to art. 167 bis itself was identified.**
  - **Loi de financement de la sécurité sociale 2026** — CSG on capital income **+1.4 points** from **1 January 2026**, taking the PFU from **30% to 31.4%** (DLA Piper and service-public links above).
  - **Loi de finances pour 2026 = loi n° 2026-103 du 19 février 2026.** → [vie-publique, dossier législatif LF 2026](https://www.vie-publique.fr/dossierlegislatif/JORFDOLE000052390038) · [budget.gouv.fr, *Loi de finances 2026*](https://www.budget.gouv.fr/reperes/loi_de_finances/articles/loi-finances-2026)
  - **Exit tax under LF 2026 — preserved, not tightened.** Per bensaid-avocats: *"La loi de finances pour 2026 n'a pas durci ce régime : l'amendement rétablissant le dégrèvement à quinze ans, voté à l'Assemblée nationale en novembre 2025, n'a pas été retenu dans le texte promulgué, et les délais de deux et cinq ans demeurent."* (link above — **law-firm source, not primary**; the underlying amendment text was not retrieved).
  - **Rejected tightening amendments (Sénat, PLF 2026, 1st reading).** Two amendments to art. 167 bis VII.2 sought to **double** the dégrèvement periods (2 → 4 years; 5 → 10 years) for taxpayers controlling a company that has received **≥ €100,000** of cumulative tax or budgetary aid since its creation. **Both were rejected** (marked *Rejeté*, with an unfavourable opinion from the commission and the government):
    - [Sénat, amendement n° I-1311, 24 November 2025](https://www.senat.fr/enseance/2025-2026/138/Amdt_I-1311.html)
    - [Sénat, amendement n° I-2509 rect. ter, 27 November 2025](https://www.senat.fr/enseance/2025-2026/138/Amdt_I-2509.html) — text and *objet* retrieved in full.
  - **Also noted:** the same political current produced a **20% tax on non-professional assets of *holdings patrimoniales*** in LF 2026 (bensaid-avocats, law-firm source, **UNVERIFIED** against primary text).
