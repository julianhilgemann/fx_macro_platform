## 1. IRPEF top rate, INPS employee contributions, surcharges

**IRPEF 2025 (tax year 2025) — 3 brackets, confirmed as structural by Legge di Bilancio 2025 (L. 207/2024):**

| Bracket (EUR) | Rate | Tax on intermediate income |
|---|---|---|
| up to 28,000 | **23%** | 23% of total |
| 28,001 – 50,000 | **35%** | 6,440 + 35% above 28,000 |
| **above 50,000** | **43% (top rate)** | **14,140 + 43% above 50,000** |

Source (official, "Dall'anno 2024 …": 23% / 35% / 43%): https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/imposta-sul-reddito-delle-persone-fisiche-irpef-/aliquote-e-calcolo-dell-irpef
English official mirror (same 23/35/43 table, last update 16/01/2026): https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/en/web/english/personal-income-tax-rates-and-calculation
Confirmation that L. 207/2024 made the three rates structural for 2025: https://www.informazionefiscale.it/IRPEF-2025-aliquote-scaglioni-come-funziona

**LEGISLATED CHANGE — tax year 2026 (L. 199/2025, Legge di Bilancio 2026, art. 1 co. 3-4):** second bracket cut **35% → 33%**; tax above 50,000 becomes **13,700 + 43%** (was 14,140 + 43%). Benefit sterilised for taxpayers with total income **> EUR 200,000** via a **EUR 440** reduction of the 19% deductions (excluding medical expenses) — new art. 16-ter co. 5-bis TUIR.
Official: https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/imposta-sul-reddito-delle-persone-fisiche-irpef-/aliquote-e-calcolo-dell-irpef ("la legge di Bilancio 2026 ha ridotto la seconda aliquota … dal 35 al 33 per cento … 13.700 euro (non più 14.140)").
MEF, 12/01/2026: "Reduction of the second IRPEF bracket (income between €28,000 and €50,000) from 35% to 33%. The reduction will not apply to incomes above €200,000." https://www.mef.gov.it/en/focus/Main-measures-of-the-2026-Budget-Law-00001/
Legislative technique + EUR 440 clawback detail: https://www.edotto.com/articolo/nuova-irpef-e-detassazione-le-novita-della-legge-di-bilancio-2026

**Employee INPS social security — tax year 2025 (FPLD, private-sector employees):**
- **9.19%** employee share on taxable pay. Source: https://leggeinchiaro.it/calcola/contributi-inps/
- **+1% additional employee contribution** (art. 3-ter D.L. 384/1992 conv. L. 438/1992) on pay **above the first pensionable bracket**: **EUR 55,448/yr** for **2025** (2026: EUR 56,224). Source: https://www.eurekaprevidenza.it/index.php/2346-retribuzione-pensionabile-e-massimale-contributivo
- **Contribution ceiling (massimale contributivo)** for workers with **no contribution seniority at 31/12/1995**: **EUR 120,607 (2025)**; 2026: EUR 122,295. Same source as above; 2026 figure independently confirmed by PwC: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/other-taxes ("For FY 2026 … cap for Gestione Separata INPS purposes is EUR 122,295"). The 9.19% + 1% are **not** capped for workers with pre-1996 seniority.
- Whole employer+employee burden ≈ **40%** of gross (≈30% employer / ≈10% employee): PwC https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/other-taxes

**Surcharges (material to the top marginal rate):**
- **Addizionale regionale:** base **1.23%** nationwide; regions may raise it — **range generally 1.23%–3.33%**. PwC: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/taxes-on-personal-income ; Chambers Private Wealth 2025 (Italy): "Local (regional and municipal) surcharges generally apply up to roughly 2%" https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Private-Wealth-2025/290/
- **Addizionale comunale:** **0%–0.9%**, municipalities may apply progressive rates on the national brackets. PwC (same URL).
- Net effect: top marginal IRPEF + surcharges ≈ **43% + up to ~1–2%** (≈44–45%); surcharges are not deductible from IRPEF. Official mechanics (surcharges computed on total income, not deductible): Agenzia delle Entrate, *Redditi PF 2026* instructions, Sez. I/II (quadro RV) https://infoprecompilata.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/10180/1848915/PF1_2026_istruzioni_DE.pdf/05a4274b-90de-fc5b-8aa6-234d0d7f2e8c?version=1.0

## 2. Capital gains — LISTED securities, resident individual (tax year 2025)

- **Headline: 26% imposta sostitutiva** on capital gains from disposal of **both qualified and non-qualified shareholdings**; applies to listed shares and other financial assets. PwC (Income determination, last reviewed 23/07/2026): "Capital gains on financial assets are generally subject to a 26% substitute tax." https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/income-determination ; Chambers Private Wealth 2025: https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Private-Wealth-2025/290/
- **No holding-period exemption for listed shares held by individuals.** No step-up/exemption on time held; the only real-estate 5-year/permanent-home exemptions are separate. PwC lists exemptions only for real estate (held >5 years; principal abode) https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/income-determination
- **12.5% on qualifying government/white-list bonds:** Italian State bonds and equivalent securities (art. 31 D.P.R. 601/1973), plus bonds of white-list States (ministerial list of 4/9/1996) and, since D.L. 66/2014, foreign local-authority bonds. Mechanism: only **48.08%** of price difference is entered in quadro RT and 26% applied → effective **12.5%**; correspondingly, losses on these securities offset only **48.08%**. Sources: Chambers 2025 (explicit "12.5% substitutive tax") https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Private-Wealth-2025/290/ ; mechanics: https://leggeinchiaro.it/plusvalenze-obbligazioni-titoli-stato-12-5/ (Note: the 12.5% applies to the **price gain**, not to coupons, which have their own regime.)
- **"2025 change to the 26% rate":** the 26% financial-gains rate was **not** changed for 2025. The 2026 Budget Law raised the rate **26% → 33% only for crypto-assets** from FY 2026 (26% retained for euro stablecoins). PwC: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/other-taxes ("Taxation of crypto-assets — The Budget Law for FY 2026 increased the tax rate from 26% to 33% on capital gains and income derived from crypto-assets."); same on https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/income-determination
- **Participation exemption for individuals:** the **PEX (participation exemption)** is a **corporate** regime (art. 87 TUIR); individuals do **not** get PEX on share disposals. For individuals, capital gains on **qualified** shareholdings are taxed at the **same 26%** as non-qualified (since the 2018 reform); the only current carve-out of note is the **24-bis flat-tax carve-out** (gains on qualified participations under art. 67(1)(c) TUIR realised in the **first 5 tax periods** of the flat-tax option stay under the ordinary regime — Fisco Oggi, 06/02/2026: https://www.fiscooggi.it/portale/-/regime-agevolato-dei-neo-residenti-maggiorati-gli-importi-forfettari).
- **PIR (Piani Individuali di Risparmio) — exemption:** full **exemption from the 26% substitute tax** (capital gains, interest, dividends) plus **exemption from inheritance tax**, if the plan is held **≥5 consecutive years** and portfolio/contribution limits are respected. Portfolio: **≥70%** in Italian/EU-with-Italian-PE issuers; of that, **≥25%** outside FTSE MIB and **≥5%** outside FTSE MIB and FTSE Mid Cap; max **10%** per single issuer. Caps: **ordinary PIR — EUR 500 to 40,000 per year, EUR 200,000 total**; **alternative PIR — EUR 300,000 per year, EUR 1.5m total**. One PIR per person. Early exit ⇒ exemption lost, tax + interest due; reinvestment of disposals within **90 days** preserves the benefit.
  Sources: Banca d'Italia (official): https://economiapertutti.bancaditalia.it/aree-tematiche/risparmio-e-investimenti/i-piani-individuali-di-risparmio-a-lungo-termine/index.html ; https://leggeinchiaro.it/pir-piani-individuali-risparmio-esenzione-730/

## 3. Dividends — resident individual (tax year 2025)

- **Headline: 26%** final withholding/substitute tax on dividends from **both qualified and non-qualified** shareholdings. PwC: "Financial income and alike – such as dividends and interests – is generally subject to a final withholding tax/substitutive tax of 26%." https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Private-Wealth-2025/290/ ; PwC Income determination: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/income-determination
- **The "58.14% × 26%" formula is NOT current law.** 58.14% was the **pre-2018** inclusion rate for **qualified** shareholdings (holding >20% of votes / >25% of equity, or >2% / >5% for listed companies) under the old art. 27 D.P.R. 917/1986 (58.14% = 40% × 1.4536 gross-up), giving an effective **≈15.1%**; the 2018 reform (L. 205/2017) ended the distinction and moved both qualified and non-qualified dividends to the **26%** substitute tax. The **49.72%** factor (40% × 1.243) applies to **non-resident** holders of qualified holdings. So: **58.14% / 49.72% survive only as transitional rules for pre-2017 profits**.
  Transitional verification source: https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/fisco-e-patrimonio/imposte-sul-reddito/regime-transitorio-dividendi-verifica-della-consistenza-della-partecipazione/ ; end of qualified/non-qualified distinction: https://ntplusfisco.ilsole24ore.com/art/circolari-24dividendi-archivio-distinzione-le-partecipazioni-AEvfxxCE
- **Tax-year caution:** dividends distributed by an Italian company out of **profits formed up to 31/12/2016** to a **qualified** holder are still subject to the transitional regime (inclusion of 58.14% × 26% — effective ≈15.12% — or 49.72% for non-residents). This is a residency/period-specific question, not a headline-rate question.
- **No "combined" 58.14% effective rate applies to a resident individual in 2025** for profits formed from 2017 onwards: the rate is a flat 26%.
- PIR-held dividends: exempt (see point 2). Foreign dividends: 26% (with foreign tax credit where applicable).

## 4. Annual net wealth tax and inheritance/gift tax

### Net wealth taxes — **NO general net wealth tax**; only asset-specific foreign-asset taxes (tax year 2025)

**IVIE (foreign real estate), resident individuals:**
- Rate **1.06% from 1 January 2024** (raised from 0.76% by **L. 213/2023, Budget Law 2024**). Currently: **1.06%** for individuals (the 0.4% figure no longer applies to individuals; 0.76% was the pre-2024 rate). PwC: "From FY 2024, the applicable tax rate is 1.06%." https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/other-taxes ; rate history (0.76 → 1.06 from 01/01/2024, L. 213/2023): https://www.fidinam.com/it/blog/manovra-2024-italia-patrimoni-esteri-sempre-piu-tassati
- **No payment if total IVIE ≤ EUR 200**; principal residence abroad excluded; foreign property tax deductible. PwC (same URL); Fidinam (same URL).
- Base: purchase cost / market value; for EU/EEA-with-exchange-of-information property, the foreign **cadastral value** if available. PwC (same URL).

**IVAFE (foreign financial assets), resident individuals:**
- Rate **0.2%** on value at 31 December. PwC: "The applicable tax rate is 0.2%." https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/other-taxes
- **0.4%** if assets held in privileged-taxation jurisdictions (doubled from 0.2% by L. 213/2023 from 01/01/2024); **Switzerland out of scope from tax period 2024**. PwC (same URL); Fidinam (same URL).
- **Bank accounts: fixed EUR 34.20 per account**, not due if the **average annual balance < EUR 5,000** — *the commonly cited "EUR 6,000 exemption" is not the current figure*. PwC (same URL): "For bank accounts, IVAFE is generally due as a fixed amount of EUR 34.20 for each bank account. This amount is not due if the average annual balance of the bank account is lower than EUR 5,000."
- Italian-situs financial assets instead bear **imposta di bollo 0.2%** (bank statements EUR 34.20 for individuals / EUR 100 for non-individuals). PwC (same URL).

### Inheritance and gift tax (imposta di successione e donazione) — official rates and thresholds

| Beneficiary | Rate | Exemption (per beneficiary) |
|---|---|---|
| Spouse / direct line (ascendants & descendants) | **4%** | **EUR 1,000,000** |
| Brothers & sisters | **6%** | **EUR 100,000** |
| Other relatives up to 4th degree; in-laws up to 3rd degree collateral | **6%** | **none** |
| **All other beneficiaries (unrelated)** | **8%** | **none** |
| Persons with severe disability (art. 3 co. 3 L. 104/1992) | (per relationship) | **EUR 1,500,000** |

Official: Agenzia delle Entrate, "Aliquote e franchigie" — https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/it/schede/pagamenti/imposta-di-successione/aliquote-e-franchigie-professionisti
Independent confirmation (same 4%/6%/8% + thresholds): PwC https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/other-taxes
**From 1 January 2025**: inheritance/gift tax is **self-assessed by the taxpayer within 90 days** of filing the inheritance tax return. PwC (same URL).

## 5. Pre-existing foreign capital for a NEW resident

- **Worldwide taxation from day one** for tax residents ("tax residents are also subject to taxation on foreign income… and to wealth taxes on real estate and financial investments owned outside Italy"). PwC: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/taxes-on-personal-income
- **No remittance basis, no general step-up on immigration.** Pre-existing foreign capital is not taxed as such, but **income and gains it produces after residence begins** are taxable in Italy (with foreign tax credit), plus IVIE/IVAFE and the **quadro RW monitoring obligation** (PwC same URL). Note the 2024 ATAD-consistent **"split-year"** residence rules (D.Lgs. 209/2023) determine *when* Italian residence starts.
- **Special regimes that displace worldwide taxation** (details in point 6): (a) art. 24-bis flat tax on foreign income; (b) art. 24-ter 7% regime for foreign pensioners; (c) impatriati/inbound-worker regime (art. 5 D.Lgs. 209/2023, 50% exemption up to EUR 600,000/yr, 5 years, 40% for those with minor children); (d) new-resident/start-up and research regimes. PwC: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/income-determination
- Monitoring exemption: opting for the art. 24-bis flat tax removes quadro RW, IVIE, IVAFE and foreign-asset inheritance/gift tax. Fisco Oggi, 06/02/2026: https://www.fiscooggi.it/portale/-/regime-agevolato-dei-neo-residenti-maggiorati-gli-importi-forfettari

## 6. Special regimes — CURRENT status (as at 17 Sept 2026)

### (a) HNWI flat tax — art. 24-bis TUIR ("neo-residenti")
**Amount history (all confirmed):**
| Transfers of residence | Annual substitute tax | Per family member |
|---|---|---|
| up to 9 Aug 2024 | **EUR 100,000** | EUR 25,000 |
| **from 10 Aug 2024** (D.L. 113/2024, in force 10/08/2024) | **EUR 200,000** | EUR 25,000 |
| **from 1 Jan 2026** (L. 199/2025, Budget Law 2026, art. 1 co. 25-26) | **EUR 300,000** | **EUR 50,000** |

- Official (Italian, last updated 16/01/2026): *"la legge di bilancio 2026 ha elevato a 300.000 euro l'importo dell'imposta sostitutiva … e a 50.000 euro … per ciascuno dei familiari … si applica alle persone fisiche che spostano la residenza fiscale nel territorio dello Stato a partire dal 1° gennaio 2026"* and *"un'imposta forfettaria di 200mila euro … (fino al 10 agosto 2024 l'importo è stato di 100mila euro)"*. https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/agevolazioni/opzione-per-i-neo-residenti/infogen-opzionale-neo-residenti-cittadini
- **Was the 2024 doubling for new applicants only, or existing ones too?** The increase was **modulated on the date the individual transferred (civil-law) residence to Italy**, i.e. it applied to *persons who transferred residence after the entry into force of D.L. 113/2024 (10 August 2024)* — **not** retroactively to those who had already transferred earlier. Express: ratioquotidiano, 10/01/2026: *"con il D.L. 113/2024 aveva elevato la soglia da 100.000 a 200.000 euro, con applicazione retroattiva modulata sulla base della data di trasferimento della residenza civilistica"* https://www.ratioquotidiano.it/flat-tax-neo-residenti-nuovo-importo-e-impatto-sul-regime-opzionale ; Orbitax: *"The increase applies to persons that have transferred their tax residence to Italy after the Decree-Law enter[ed into force]"* (D.L. 113 of 9 Aug 2024, in force 10 Aug 2024) https://stage.orbitax.com/news/country/article/Update---Italy-Publishes-Decre-56434
- **Existing/earlier optants keep their old amount.** PwC (last reviewed 23/07/2026): *"individuals who opted for the regime from the 2024 tax period may continue to pay the flat tax of EUR 100,000 until the end of the application period; individuals who opted for the regime from the 2025 tax period may continue to pay the flat tax of EUR 200,000; as per the changes introduced by the 2026 Italian Budget Law, for individuals who transfer their legal residency to Italy from 1 January 2026, the flat tax is increased to EUR 300,000."* https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/taxes-on-personal-income
- **Requirement: non-Italian tax residence for at least 9 of the 10 tax periods preceding the option's first year.** Official: Agenzia delle Entrate (IT) and PwC (same URLs above).
- **Duration: 15 years** from the first tax period of validity; tacitly renewed annually; ending on revocation, forfeiture (e.g. non-payment, moving residence abroad) or after 15 years. Official: Agenzia delle Entrate (same URL). Single payment, no *ravvedimento operoso*; F24 code **NRPP** (refund/offset code **NRRE**).
- **EU/EEA abridged re-entry (4 years):** the "9 out of 10" test is relaxed for EU/EEA nationals — **UNVERIFIED at primary-source level in this session** (see UNVERIFIED list). The 9/10 test itself is verified.
- **Carve-out:** gains on **qualified participations** (art. 67(1)(c) TUIR) realised in the **first 5 tax periods** of the option remain under the ordinary regime. Fisco Oggi, 06/02/2026 (same URL).
- **Cumulation ban with impatriati:** confirmed by the conversion law of the *decreto fiscale* (D.L. 38/2026), published in **Gazzetta Ufficiale n. 117 of 22 May 2026**: joint application of art. 5 D.Lgs. 209/2023 and art. 24-bis remains possible **until 31 December 2026**; **from 1 January 2027** the ban is fully operative for those transferring residence from tax period 2027. https://www.studiociulla.ateneoweb.com/aw/print/regime-impatriati-e-flat-tax-neo-residenti-confermato-il-divieto-di-cumulo-dal-2027,news-47442-1.pdf
- **Stale official English page (caution):** the Invest-in-Italy English page still shows **EUR 100,000 / EUR 25,000 / 15 years** and has not been updated. https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/en/web/english/nse/invest-in-italy/tax-regime-for-new-residents — do not rely on it.

### (b) EUR 100,000 "neo-resident" flat tax for transfers to the Mezzogiorno
**No such regime is currently in force, and none was found in current law (repealed/never enacted).** Italian law provides exactly two flat-tax regimes for inbound individuals: **art. 24-bis** (HNWI, EUR 300,000 from 2026, nationwide) and **art. 24-ter** (7% for foreign pensioners in the South). A **EUR 100,000 southern-Italy flat tax** appears to refer to the **pre-August-2024 art. 24-bis amount** (which was never geographically restricted) or to a **draft/rumoured** measure. Express searches for a live southern EUR 100,000 regime returned no official source. Status: **not available to new applicants in 2025/2026** (UNVERIFIED as to whether a proposal existed — see list).
Sources confirming the only two regimes and their geography: Agenzia delle Entrate neo-residenti page (no geographic condition) https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/agevolazioni/opzione-per-i-neo-residenti/infogen-opzionale-neo-residenti-cittadini ; Agenzia delle Entrate pensioners page (southern municipalities) https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/web/guest/schede/agevolazioni/regime-opzionale-per-i-pensionati-esteri-che-cose-cittadini

### (c) 7% flat tax for foreign pensioners in southern Italy — art. 24-ter TUIR
- **Still open to new applicants**; rate **7%**; on **all foreign-source income** (not only pensions: capital income, financial income, rental, employment/self-employment where conditions met). Official (last updated **8 May 2026**): https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/web/guest/schede/agevolazioni/regime-opzionale-per-i-pensionati-esteri-che-cose-cittadini
- **Municipality population: raised from 20,000 to 30,000 inhabitants, in force from 7 April 2026** (official: *"tale limite è in vigore dal 7 aprile 2026, in precedenza era 20mila abitanti"*). Carried by **Legge annuale PMI, L. 11 marzo 2026, n. 34**, published in Gazzetta Ufficiale 23 March 2026. Sources: Agenzia delle Entrate (same URL); https://ildiritto.it/pensioni/legge-pmi-flat-tax-al-7-per-le-pensioni-estere/ ; https://www.wallstreetitalia.com/flat-tax-per-chi-si-trasferisce-al-sud-cosa-cambia-dal-7-aprile-2026/
- **Duration: 9 tax periods** per the Agenzia's current wording (*"per ciascuno dei nove periodi d'imposta di validità dell'opzione"*), i.e. the year of transfer + 9 (10 years in substance; PwC states "*valid from that tax period and for the following nine*"). Agenzia (same URL); PwC https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/taxes-on-personal-income
- **Conditions:** holder of foreign pension income; **not Italian tax resident for at least 5 tax periods** before the option; residence transferred to a qualifying southern municipality (Sicily, Calabria, Sardinia, Campania, Basilicata, Abruzzo, Molise, Puglia), *plus* the 2009 L'Aquila / 2016 Central Italy earthquake municipalities **with no population limit**; transfer from a State with administrative cooperation agreement with Italy. PwC (same URL); Agenzia (same URL).
- **Also useful:** the flat tax **excludes** these taxpayers from the **quadro RW** monitoring obligation (per PwC/Italian practice) and IVIE/IVAFE is commonly stated as covered — treat the IVIE/IVAFE exclusion as **UNVERIFIED** here (the Agenzia page fetched does not state it).
- **Note:** the current Agenzia page does **not** mention the option of excluding specific jurisdictions; that detail was found only in a secondary source (ildiritto, same URL) and in PwC.

## 7. Exit tax when leaving Italy

- **Individuals: no exit tax on private wealth.** Art. 166 TUIR ("Imposizione in uscita") applies only to **subjects exercising a commercial business** (transfer abroad of tax residence of a sole proprietorship/company, transfer of assets to a foreign PE, etc.) — implementing **art. 5 of Directive (EU) 2016/1164 (ATAD)**. Text: https://leggeinchiaro.it/articolo-166-del-tuir/ ; confirmation that private individuals/investors are outside it: https://quifinanza.it/fisco-tasse/exit-tax-persone-fisiche-trasferimento-residenza-europa/1013430/ ("In Italia non c'è una exit tax per chi si trasferisce all'estero … il sistema fiscale italiano non prevede alcuna exit tax per le persone fisiche"; listed shares, non-qualified holdings, fund units, crypto can be moved with no taxation of latent gains)
- **Deferral for business exit tax (art. 166 TUIR):** suspension of the tax and **instalments over 5 tax periods** (or **6** in certain cases), with the first instalment due in the year of the transfer and **sureties** required; per art. 166 co. 4 and the MEF decree of 2/7/2014 (G.U. 8/7/2014 n. 156). See art. 166 text (same URL). **Exact instalment count and guarantee conditions: UNVERIFIED at primary-source article level in this session.**
- **"Law 178/2020" — not an individual exit tax.** L. 178/2020 (Budget Law 2021) art. 1 co. 595 dealt with other matters; **no individual unrealised-gains exit tax was introduced by it**. **UNVERIFIED** whether any L. 178/2020 provision was intended.
- **Practical point for the leaving individual:** the **art. 24-bis** flat tax and the **7% art. 24-ter** regime **cease** if the taxpayer transfers tax residence abroad (express ground of cessation for 24-bis: Agenzia delle Entrate, neo-residenti page: *"per … trasferimento della residenza fiscale in altro Stato o territorio"*). https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/agevolazioni/opzione-per-i-neo-residenti/infogen-opzionale-neo-residenti-cittadini
- **Any Italian "exit tax"/"wealth tax on leaving" proposal (2025–2026):** none found in official sources.

## 8. Annual minimum tax / lump-sum floors at MODERATE wealth

- **No wealth threshold for the HNWI flat tax** — it is a **pure lump-sum floor**: **EUR 200,000/yr** (transfers 10/08/2024–31/12/2025) or **EUR 300,000/yr** (from 01/01/2026), payable **in a single instalment**, **no *ravvedimento operoso***, **not deductible** from any other tax or contribution, regardless of the amount of foreign income (EUR 300,000 even on EUR 50,000 of foreign income). Agenzia delle Entrate: https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/agevolazioni/opzione-per-i-neo-residenti/infogen-opzionale-neo-residenti-cittadini ; Fisco Oggi 06/02/2026 (single payment, non-deductible, no ravvedimento): https://www.fiscooggi.it/portale/-/regime-agevolato-dei-neo-residenti-maggiorati-gli-importi-forfettari
- **Family members:** **+EUR 50,000 each** (from 2026; EUR 25,000 before) — a second lump-sum floor, not income-related. Same sources.
- **IVIE de minimis:** not due if total **≤ EUR 200**; **no** floor otherwise (whole amount due). PwC https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/italy/individual/other-taxes
- **IVAFE:** 0.2% with **no de minimis for securities**; **bank accounts**: fixed **EUR 34.20**, not due if average annual balance **< EUR 5,000**. PwC (same URL).
- **Imposta di bollo on Italian financial assets: 0.2%** (no floor), bank statement duty EUR 34.20. PwC (same URL).
- **Entry-cost reality check:** the flat tax is a **floor unrelated to wealth** — it is uneconomic below roughly EUR 600k–1m of foreign income (EUR 300k tax = 30% of EUR 1m) — but that is arithmetic, not a rule.
- **Crypto:** 26% → **33% from FY 2026** (26% for euro stablecoins). PwC (same URL).
