## United States

**1. Rule & citation**
- Mark-to-market exit tax: **IRC §877A**, enacted by Pub. L. 110-245 (HEART Act 2008) §301(a), applicable to expatriation dates on/after 17 Jun 2008 — https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/877A
- Reporting: **Form 8854** (initial + annual), IRC §6039G — https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8854

**2. Thresholds — exact figures, both years**
- **§877(a)(2)(A) average annual net income tax** (5 tax years ending before expatriation):
  - **2025: more than $206,000** — Rev. Proc. 2024-40, §4.37 — https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-24-40.pdf
  - **2026: more than $211,000** — Rev. Proc. 2025-32, §4.37 — https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf
- **§877A(a)(3) exclusion amount** (per person, reduces includible gain but not below zero):
  - **2025: $890,000** — Rev. Proc. 2024-40, §4.38 — https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-24-40.pdf ✅ (the commonly cited $890,000 is **confirmed correct for 2025**)
  - **2026: $910,000** — Rev. Proc. 2025-32, §4.38 — https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf
  - Corroborated by IRS Instructions for Form 8854 (2025), "What's New" — https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8854
- **Net worth test: $2,000,000 or more**, statutory and **not** inflation-indexed — §877(a)(2)(B) — https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/877
- **Certification test**: failure to certify 5 years of compliance under penalty of perjury — §877(a)(2)(C) (same URL)
- Exceptions (dual citizen at birth; relinquishment before age 18½ with ≤10 US resident years) — §877A(g)(1)(B) — https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/877A
- Long-term resident = lawful permanent resident in **8 of the last 15** tax years — §877(e)(2) — https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/877

**3. Rate & base**
- **§877A(a)(1)–(2)**: all property treated as sold the day before the expatriation date at FMV; gains taken into account that year; §1091 wash-sale rules disapplied for losses.
- **No flat exit-tax rate** — the gain is taxed under §1 or §55 (ordinary/AMT) as applicable; the §877A(a)(3) exclusion ($890k/2025, $910k/2026) shelters the first slice.
- **Carve-outs from mark-to-market** — §877A(c): deferred compensation items (§877A(d) — **30% withholding** on eligible items), specified tax deferred accounts (§877A(e) — IRAs, §529, §529A ABLE, Coverdell ESA, HSA, Archer MSA treated as distributed), nongrantor trust interests (§877A(f) — **30% withholding** on the taxable portion).
- Basis step-up to FMV for property held on the date of first becoming a US resident — §877A(h)(2).

**4. Deferral**
- **§877A(b)**: per-property, irrevocable election; payment extended to the due date of the return for the year the property is disposed of; requires **adequate security** (bond meeting §6325, or letter of credit) — (b)(4); irrevocable treaty-rights waiver — (b)(5); termination at death — (b)(3); **interest continues to accrue under §6601** — (b)(7). All: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/877A
- IRS no longer requires pre-contact to arrange security, but contact is required to satisfy the liability — https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8854

**5. 2025 / 2026 changes**
- **No legislated amendment to §877A** in 2025 or 2026 — the 2025 Form 8854 "What's New" lists only the inflation increases and new §2801 guidance — https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8854
- §2801 covered gifts/bequests annual exclusion: **$19,000 for calendar 2026** — Rev. Proc. 2025-32, §4.42(3) — https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf

## Canada

**1. Rule & citation**
- **Income Tax Act (R.S.C. 1985, c. 1 (5th Supp.)) s. 128.1(4)** — deemed disposition on ceasing to be resident; **s. 128.1(4)(b)** is the operative provision. Act current to 21 Jul 2026 — https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/section-128.1.html

**2. Threshold / trigger**
- **There is no net-worth or income threshold.** The deemed disposition applies to any individual who ceases to be resident. No CAD 250,000 threshold exists anywhere in s. 128.1 — **UNVERIFIED / NOT FOUND** (see Uncertain).
- **Reporting threshold only: > CAD 25,000** — s. 128.1(9) requires a list of reportable property where total FMV immediately after departure exceeds **$25,000** (Form T1161) — same URL.
- **Short-stay relief** — s. 128.1(4)(b)(iv): property owned when the individual last became resident (or acquired by inheritance/bequest thereafter) is excluded if the individual was **not resident in Canada for more than 60 months** in the 120-month period ending at departure.

**3. Rate & base**
- Deemed disposal at FMV immediately before departure (proceeds deemed received then) — s. 128.1(4)(b); deemed reacquisition at that FMV — s. 128.1(4)(c) — https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/section-128.1.html
- **Excluded from the deemed disposition** (individuals): real or immovable property **situated in Canada**, Canadian resource property, timber resource property — (4)(b)(i); capital property/Class 14.1 property/inventory of a business carried on through a PE in Canada — (4)(b)(ii); "excluded right or interest" — (4)(b)(iii).
- **Excluded rights or interests** (s. 128.1(10)) include RRSP, RRIF, RESP, **RDSP, TFSA, FHSA**, DPSP, EPSP, RPP, RCA, employee benefit plans, CPP/OAS rights, certain personal-trust and life-insurance interests. **Principal residence:** because real property situated in Canada is excluded by (4)(b)(i), the Canadian home is not caught by the departure deemed disposition; the principal residence exemption applies on actual sale.
- **Rate:** no special exit-tax rate — the deemed gain is included in income for the departure year under ordinary rules; capital gains inclusion rate **50%**.
- Related: post-emigration loss election s. 128.1(8); election to unwind the deemed disposition on return s. 128.1(6); part-year residency s. 114; instalment-interest relief s. 128.1(5) (all in the same URL).

**4. Deferral / payment**
- **s. 220(4.5) — "Security for departure tax"**: an individual deemed by s. 128.1(4) to have disposed of property may elect, in prescribed manner **on or before the balance-due day for the emigration year**, for the Minister to accept **adequate security**; per (4.5)(b), interest and penalties are **computed as if the secured amount had been paid on account** — https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/section-220.html
- Supporting provisions: **s. 220(4.51)** deemed security for small amounts (capped by reference to tax a Canadian-resident trust with $50,000 taxable income would pay); **s. 220(4.52)** limit; **s. 220(4.53)** inadequate security with 90-day cure; **s. 220(4.54)** ministerial extension; **s. 220(4.7)** undue hardship relief — same URL.
- Form: **T1244**, *Election under subsection 220(4.5) … to defer payment of income tax related to a deemed disposition* — https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/formspubs/pbg/t1244/t1244-fill-22e.pdf
- Form: **T1243**, *Deemed Disposition of Property by an Emigrant of Canada* — https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/formspubs/pbg/t1243/t1243-fill-22e.pdf
- Form: **T1161**, *List of Properties by an Emigrant of Canada* — https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/formspubs/pbg/t1161/t1161-fill-22e.pdf

**5. 2025 / 2026 changes**
- **Capital gains inclusion-rate increase cancelled — 21 March 2025.** The proposed rise to 66.67% (announced Budget 2024, deferred to 2026) was cancelled; the inclusion rate remains **50%**. Prime Minister's news release — https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2025/03/21/prime-minister-mark-carney-cancels-proposed-capital-gains-tax-increase
- Lifetime capital gains exemption **maintained at $1,250,000** on small business shares and farming/fishing property — same URL.
- CRA has confirmed the s. 220(4.5)(a)(i) departure-tax deferral is available for AMT payable because of s. 128.1(4)(b) — https://taxinterpretations.com/content/1062484 (secondary).
- **No amendment to s. 128.1 in 2025 or 2026 identified.**

## Japan

**1. Rule & citation**
- **国外転出時課税制度** ("taxation on departure from Japan"). Statutory basis listed by the NTA: **所法60の2、60の3、130、137の2、151の2、153の2、所令170、266の2、措法93、復興財確法13** — i.e. Income Tax Act **Art. 60-2** (and 60-3 for gift/inheritance), Special Taxation Measures Act Art. 93.
- NTA Tax Answer **No.1478** (法令等 current as of **1 April 2026**) — https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1478.htm
- NTA programme page — https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/shinkoku/kokugai/01.htm
- Created by the **平成27年度税制改正**; applies to departures, gifts, inheritances and bequests on/after **1 July 2015** (same NTA URL).

**2. Threshold / trigger — both tests must be met**
- **(a) JPY 100,000,000 (1億円) or more** of **対象資産** owned/contracted at the time of departure. Target assets = **有価証券** (securities — shares, investment trusts, etc.), 匿名組合契約の出資の持分, **未決済信用取引** and **未決済デリバティブ取引** (unsettled margin and derivative transactions, measured on a deemed-settlement basis) — https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1478.htm
- **(b) 5-of-10-years residence test**: the total period during which the individual had a **住所 or 居所 (domicile or residence) in Japan within the 10 years before departure exceeds 5 years** — same URL.
- Applies to **foreign nationals** as well as Japanese nationals; practitioner commentary notes the 5-of-10 test meant most foreign nationals only became caught from **1 July 2020** — https://leowealth.global/insights/everything-you-need-to-know-about-japan-exit-tax/ (secondary).
- **Not** in scope: cash, bank deposits, **cryptocurrencies**, real estate and other real property — same secondary URL (**UNVERIFIED** against an NTA statement of exclusion).

**3. Rate & base**
- Deemed sale/settlement of target assets **at the time of departure**, with income tax on the unrealised gain — https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1478.htm
- **Rate 15.315%** = 15% national income tax + **2.1% 復興特別所得税 (reconstruction surtax)** ⇒ 15% × 1.021 = 15.315%. The NTA confirms the surtax is filed and paid alongside income tax and is 2.1% of 基準所得税額 — same URL. Corroborated: https://leowealth.global/insights/everything-you-need-to-know-about-japan-exit-tax/
- Filing mechanics: with a 納税管理人 notified before departure → 確定申告 and payment by the ordinary filing deadline; **without** one → **準確定申告** and payment **before** departure — same NTA URL.
- Local inhabitant tax (個人住民税) on departure gains — **UNVERIFIED** (not addressed on the NTA page cited).

**4. Deferral — 納税猶予の特例**
- **5 years** from the departure date; extendable to a maximum of **10 years** by filing the 期限延長届出書. Due date = 4 months after the day following expiry of the deferral period — https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1478.htm
- Conditions: (i) notify the **納税管理人** to the tax office **before** departure; (ii) state the deferral application in the return; (iii) attach the specified 明細書 and 計算書; (iv) **provide security (担保)** for the deferred tax **and 利子税 (interest)** by the filing deadline — same URL.
- Annual **継続適用届出書** by 15 March each year while the assets are held — same URL.
- Termination: partial on disposal/gift (4-month rule); full on failure to file the continuation notice, inadequate security, or tax-manager problems; **利子税 is payable** on the deferred amount — same URL.
- **Cancellation of the tax** if the individual returns within 5 years (10 if extended) and still holds the assets, by 更正の請求 within 4 months of return; also on gift to a resident within 5 years, or death within 5 years where all heirs become residents — same URL.

**5. 2025 / 2026 changes**
- **No change to the exit tax itself.** A full scan of the MOF **令和7年度 (FY2025)** and **令和8年度 (FY2026)** 税制改正の大綱 found **no** amendment to 国外転出時課税, the JPY 100m threshold, the target-asset definition, the 5-of-10-year test or the deferral regime. The only "国外転出" reference in the FY2025 outline is in the consumption-tax duty-free shopping section, unrelated.
  - https://www.mof.go.jp/tax_policy/tax_reform/outline/fy2025/07taikou_04.htm
  - https://www.mof.go.jp/tax_policy/tax_reform/outline/fy2026/08taikou_mokuji.htm
- **Surtax restructuring — 令和8年度税制改正の大綱 (cabinet decision 26 Dec 2025), effective 令和9年 (2027) onward:**
  - New **防衛特別所得税 (Defence Special Income Tax, tentative name)** at **1% of 基準所得税額**, for 令和9年 onwards "for the time being".
  - **復興特別所得税 cut from 2.1% to 1.1%**, period extended to **令和29年 (2047)** (previously 令和19年/2037). Applies to 令和9年分 onwards.
  - Net combined surtax = **2.1%** ⇒ the **15.315% effective rate on exit-tax gains is unchanged**.
  - https://www.mof.go.jp/tax_policy/tax_reform/outline/fy2026/08taikou_06.htm ; brochure https://www.mof.go.jp/tax_policy/publication/brochure/zeisei2026_pdf/zeisei26_05.pdf
- **"出国税" (国際観光旅客税, International Tourist Tax) raised from JPY 1,000 to JPY 3,000 per departure, effective 1 July 2026** — a flat levy on travellers, **NOT** the capital-gains exit tax.
  - https://news.web.nhk/newsweb/na/na-k10015165101000
  - http://english.news.cn/asiapacific/20260701/9ca5a640e51c42a4afc5d353a6e60eb9/c.html
- **非上場株式 (unlisted share) valuation rules** — the NTA is revising the 財産評価基本通達; earliest application **令和10年 (2028)**. This would raise appraised values of unlisted shares and therefore increase exit tax on them.
  - https://www.zaikyo.or.jp/news/n126207.html
  - https://www.lotus21.co.jp/ta/2605kffv/1120_09.pdf

## Australia

**1. Rule & citation**
- **No general exit tax and no wealth/net-worth threshold.** Australia has no separate exit-tax statute. What exists is a **CGT deemed disposal on ceasing to be an Australian tax resident**, for assets that are **not** taxable Australian property: **CGT event I1, ITAA 1997 s 104-160**.
  - https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html
- **Correction to the brief's citations:** TAP is defined in **s 855-15** (not s 855-45). **s 855-45** is *"Individual or company becomes an Australian resident"* (the re-entry rule). See the Division 855 table of provisions.

**2. Threshold / trigger**
- Trigger = **ceasing to be an Australian resident** (s 104-160(1)); time of event is when residency stops — (2).
- **s 104-160(3)**: work out gain/loss for each CGT asset owned just before the event, **except** an asset that is taxable Australian property covered by **item 1 or 3** of the s 855-15 table, or an option/right to acquire such an asset (item 4).
- **s 104-160(4)**: gain if market value > cost base; loss if market value < reduced cost base.
- **s 104-160(5)**: disregarded if the asset was acquired **before 20 September 1985** (pre-CGT).
- **Taxable Australian Property — s 855-15, five categories:**
  1. **Taxable Australian real property** (s 855-20) — real property situated in Australia (including leases of Australian land); mining, quarrying or prospecting rights over minerals/petroleum/quarry materials situated in Australia.
  2. An **indirect Australian real property interest** (s 855-25) not covered by item 5.
  3. A CGT asset **used at any time in carrying on a business through a permanent establishment in Australia** (not covered by items 1, 2 or 5).
  4. An **option or right to acquire** a CGT asset covered by item 1, 2 or 3.
  5. A CGT asset covered by **s 104-165(3)** — i.e. an asset over which the individual made the choice to disregard the I1 gain.
  - https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_9/document_9.html
- Trusts: **CGT event I2** where a trust stops being a resident trust — s 104-170 (same document_3 URL).

**3. Rate & base**
- Deemed disposal at **market value**; **no special exit-tax rate** — the gain is included in assessable income and taxed at the individual's marginal rates (plus Medicare levy). The 50% CGT discount was denied to foreign residents for foreign-resident accrual periods from **8 May 2012** (Subdiv 115-C apportionment) — https://austax.tools/tax-insights/cgt-discount-reform-expats-foreign-residents-2027/ (secondary).
- **An Australian expat who remains an Australian tax resident suffers no CGT event I1** and keeps resident rates and the main residence exemption — same source.

**4. Deferral — the s 104-165 choice**
- **s 104-165(2)**: *"If you are an individual, you can choose to disregard making a capital gain or a capital loss from all CGT assets covered by CGT event I1."*
- **There is no 5-year limitation on the choice in the current law.** Current s 104-165 contains only subsections **(2)** and **(3)**. The 5-year rule survives only as a **transitional** provision — **s 104-166 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997** — for an individual who was in Australia on **6 April 2006**, remained an Australian resident until ceasing, and **was an Australian resident for less than 5 years during the 10 years** before ceasing (note appended to s 104-165).
  - https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html
- **s 104-165(3)**: if the choice is made, each such asset is taken to be **taxable Australian property** until the earlier of (a) a CGT event involving ceasing to own the asset, or (b) again becoming an Australian resident. Practically the choice is irrevocable and permanently taints the assets — same URL.
- No separate statutory exit-tax instalment regime identified — **UNVERIFIED** (ATO unreachable).

**5. Main residence exemption**
- **Treasury Laws Amendment (Reducing Pressure on Housing Affordability Measures) Act 2019 (Act No. 129 of 2019)**, assented 12 December 2019 — removes access to the CGT main residence exemption for **foreign residents** for CGT events **after 30 June 2020**.
  - https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2019A00129/asmade/2019-12-12/text
- **Transitional relief**: applies where the CGT event occurs **on or before 30 June 2020** and the ownership interest was held throughout the period from just before **7:30pm ACT legal time on 9 May 2017** to the CGT event.
  - KPMG GMS Flash Alert 2019-164 — https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/xx/pdf/2019/10/fa19-164.pdf
- **Life-events exception**: the exemption can still apply where the individual has been a **foreign resident for a continuous period of 6 years or less** and a specified life event occurred in that period — terminal medical condition, death of a spouse or minor child, divorce/legal separation, compulsory acquisition.
  - CFS FirstTech — https://www.cfs.com.au/content/dam/colonial-first-state/docs/adviser/firsttech/ft-main-residence-foreign-residents.pdf
- **Status 2025/2026: unchanged — the denial remains in force** and was not restored by the 2026 CGT reform.
- **Foreign resident CGT withholding (FRCGW)**: from **1 January 2025**, rate **15%** of gross sale price (up from 12.5%) and the **$750,000 threshold abolished** — https://austax.tools/tax-insights/cgt-discount-reform-expats-foreign-residents-2027/ (secondary — ATO primary unreachable).

**6. Key 2025 / 2026 changes**
- **Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 (Act No. 49 of 2026)** — verified on the Federal Register — plus companion **Income Tax Rates Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 (Act No. 50 of 2026)**.
