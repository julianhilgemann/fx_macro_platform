|---|---|
| 0 – 19,500 | 0% |
| 19,501 – 28,000 | 20% |
| 28,001 – 36,300 | 25% |

| 22,001 – 32,000 | 20% | 2,000 |

Sources: [PwC WWTS](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/taxes-on-personal-income) ("as of 1 January 2026"); [Grant Thornton, *Cyprus Tax Reform 2026*, Jan 2026](https://www.grantthornton.com.cy/globalassets/1.-member-firms/cyprus/shareable-pdf/grant-thornton-cyprus---tax-reform-package-2026.11.pdf); [Treppides guide](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf); [Kendris, 9 Jan 2026](https://www.kendris.com/de/news-insights/2026/01/09/cyprus-tax-reform-2026/); enacted text [Law 244(I)/2025](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2025_1_244.pdf).
- **Your premise items checked:** top rate stays **35%**; the top band starts **above EUR 72,000**, not EUR 80,000. **No 42% or 45% top rate exists** in the enacted law (see UNVERIFIED). Tax-free threshold is **EUR 22,000** (announced by FinMin Keravnos on 29 Oct 2025 as an increase "from €19,500 to €22,000" — [CNA](https://aman-alliance.org/Home/ContentDetail/96554)); **no EUR 20,700/21,000 threshold** found.
- Other 2026 PIT changes (all 1 Jan 2026): per-parent child deductions **EUR 1,000 / 1,250 / 1,500** (1st/2nd/3rd+), family-income caps EUR 100,000 (≤2 children) / 150,000 (3–4) / 200,000 (5+), EUR 40,000 single person; housing deduction up to **EUR 2,000** (rent/mortgage interest, main residence); green-transition deduction up to **EUR 1,000** (carry-forward 4 years); natural-disaster insurance up to **EUR 500**; ex-gratia termination payments: **20% flat above EUR 200,000**; foreign pension 5% flat above **EUR 5,000** (was EUR 3,420) ([Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf); [PwC DTU N-1/2026](https://www.pwc.com.cy/en/publications/direct-tax-updates-2025/dtu-n1-2026.pdf); [PwC income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/income-determination)).

## 2. CGT on listed securities — exempt (confirmed, unchanged by the reform)

- **CGT rate 20%**, and it applies **only** to gains on disposal of **immovable property situated in Cyprus** and of **shares in companies that (directly or indirectly) own Cyprus immovable property**, when not subject to income tax ([PwC WWTS – other taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/other-taxes)).
- **"Shares listed on any recognised stock exchange are excluded from CGT"** — [PwC WWTS – other taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/other-taxes).
- **"Profits from disposals of corporate 'titles' are unconditionally exempt from PIT"** (titles = shares, bonds, debentures, options, CIS/UCITS/REIT units, etc.) — [PwC WWTS – income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/income-determination).
- **SDC**: SDC is charged **only on dividends, interest (and, until 31 Dec 2025, rents)** — [PwC WWTS – taxes on personal income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/taxes-on-personal-income). Capital gains on securities are therefore **outside SDC**; there was no 17% SDC charge on such gains to exempt. What the 17% hit was **dividends** (and interest), not gains.
- **2026 CGT changes** (Law 242(I)/2025, 1 Jan 2026): property-rich threshold for *indirect* holdings cut from **50% → 20%** of value derived from Cyprus immovable property; lifetime exemptions raised — general **EUR 17,086 → 30,000**, agricultural land **EUR 25,629 → 50,000**, principal residence **EUR 85,430 → 150,000** (overall lifetime cap EUR 85,430 → 150,000); property exchanges extended to consideration in kind ([PwC WWTS – other taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/other-taxes); [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf); [Andersen Cyprus](https://cy.andersen.com/news/cyprus-news-cyprus-tax-reform-comprehensive-update-effective-from-1-january-2026/); [Law 242(I)/2025](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2025_1_242.pdf)). **No change to the securities/titles exemption** (only from 1 Jan 2031 will redemption of units/shares in CIS in corporate form be treated as a dividend — [PwC income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/income-determination)).

## 3. Dividends — SDC cut 17% → 5%, NOT abolished

- **2025**: dividends exempt from income tax for individuals but subject to **SDC 17%** where the individual is **both Cyprus tax resident and Cyprus domiciled** ([PwC WWTS – taxes on personal income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)).
- **From 1 Jan 2026**: SDC on dividends for resident+domiciled individuals **reduced to 5%** (s.3(1)(a) SDC Law 117(I)/2002 as amended by **Law 245(I)/2025**, Gaz. 5070, 31.12.2025) — [Kleanthous & Platis, 18 Aug 2026](https://www.kleanthousplatis.com/news-publications/non-dom-what-it-exempts); [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.cy/globalassets/1.-member-firms/cyprus/shareable-pdf/grant-thornton-cyprus---tax-reform-package-2026.11.pdf); [PwC WWTS](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/taxes-on-personal-income).
- **Transitional 17%**: dividends received from a Cyprus resident company **within 6 years of the amending law's commencement** (i.e. to 31 Dec 2031) **out of profits of tax years up to and including 2025** remain at **17%** (proviso (ii) to s.3(1)(a)) — [Kleanthous & Platis](https://www.kleanthousplatis.com/news-publications/non-dom-what-it-exempts); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.cy/globalassets/1.-member-firms/cyprus/shareable-pdf/grant-thornton-cyprus---tax-reform-package-2026.11.pdf); [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf).
- **SDC on interest: NOT abolished — still 17%** (3% for Cyprus/EU government bonds and listed corporate bonds; EU government bonds added 1 Jan 2026) — [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf); [PwC WWTS](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/taxes-on-personal-income). **SDC on rents abolished** from 1 Jan 2026 (was 3% on 75% of rent = 2.25% effective) — [Kleanthous & Platis](https://www.kleanthousplatis.com/news-publications/non-dom-what-it-exempts); [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf).
- **New**: disguised-dividend distribution SDC **10%**; deemed dividend distribution (DDD) **abolished** for profits from 1 Jan 2026, with transitional DDD still applying to 2024 and 2025 profits (crystallising 31/12/2026 and 31/12/2027 at 17%) — [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf); [Kendris](https://www.kendris.com/de/news-insights/2026/01/09/cyprus-tax-reform-2026/).
- **Effective combined rate, Cyprus-domiciled resident individual:** **5%** on 2026+ profits, no further income tax on dividends, **plus GHS 2.65%** if within the EUR 180,000 ceiling (total ≈ **7.65%** on 2026+ dividends below the ceiling). **Non-dom resident: 0% SDC** (GHS 2.65% still applies).

## 4. Net wealth tax and inheritance/estate tax — both nil

- **No annual net wealth tax**: PwC quick chart lists Cyprus **"NA"** (last reviewed 4 Aug 2026) — [PwC Net wealth/worth tax rates](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/net-wealth-worth-tax-rates).
- **Immovable property tax withdrawn from 1 Jan 2017** — official Tax Department page: "Ο φόρος ακίνητης ιδιοκτησίας εφαρμόστηκε την 01/01/1980 και αποσύρθηκε από 01/01/2017" ([gov.cy – Φόρος Ακίνητης Ιδιοκτησίας](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/foros-akinitis-idioktisias/); also [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/other-taxes): "IPT has been abolished as of 1 January 2017").
- **Estate duty abolished for deaths on or after 1 Jan 2000** by the Estate Duty (Amending) Law, **Law 74(I)/2000**, Official Gazette No. 3413 of **16 June 2000** (s.2 repeals the basic law in relation to persons who died on/after 1 Jan 2000) — official text: [cylaw.org 2000_1_074.pdf](http://cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2000_1_074.pdf). PwC quick chart: Cyprus inheritance tax **"NA"**; gift tax **"Nil if up to a third degree relative"** ([PwC Inheritance and gift tax rates](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/inheritance-and-gift-tax-rates), last reviewed 4 Aug 2026).
- **What applies instead**: no inheritance tax, no gift tax, no annual wealth tax; **no Land Registry transfer fees on inheritance**; probate under the Administration of Estates Law Cap. 189 with court/legal/professional fees (no ad valorem statutory probate fee verified; typical timeline 12–24 months); succession is subject to **forced heirship** under the Wills and Succession Law Cap. 195 (spouse+children: up to 75% legally reserved, 25% disposable; EU Succession Regulation 650/2012 allows a foreign national to elect home-country law); **CGT 20% can apply on a later disposal by the heir** — [index.cy Cyprus inheritance guide, July 2026](https://index.cy/el/articles/inheritance-tax-cyprus-guide/); [gk-lawfirm, updated 2 Sept 2026](https://gk-lawfirm.com/practice-areas/cyprus-citizenship-by-investment-program/).

## 5. Pre-existing foreign capital for a NEW resident

- **Worldwide taxation from day one** for Cyprus tax residents; non-residents taxed only on Cyprus-source income — [PwC WWTS – taxes on personal income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/taxes-on-personal-income); [Oxford Tax Facts 2026, p.3](https://oxfordglobalservices.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/tax-facts-2026.pdf).
- **Non-dom exemption (since 16 July 2015)**: "individuals who are not domiciled in Cyprus for SDC purposes are exempt from SDC. Thus, dividend and (most types of) interest income for such individuals are exempt from all taxes in Cyprus" — [PwC WWTS – income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/income-determination); [PwC WWTS – taxes on personal income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/taxes-on-personal-income).
- **Exact scope**: exemption is from **SDC only** — i.e. dividends and interest (and rents, which left SDC for everyone on 1 Jan 2026). No SDC on foreign-source dividends/interest for a resident non-dom; no Cyprus tax on foreign capital gains (CGT covers only Cyprus immovable property/property-rich shares); no wealth or inheritance tax. **Not exempt**: income tax (worldwide), and **GHS 2.65% on dividends/interest/rental income regardless of domicile** — the Tax Department states tax domicile "does not affect" GESY ([gov.cy GESY page](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/geniko-systima-ygeias-gesy/); [Kleanthous & Platis: s.19(1)(ζ) of Law 89(I)/2001 charges 2.65% and names dividends expressly](https://www.kleanthousplatis.com/news-publications/non-dom-what-it-exempts)).
- **Anti-abuse**: GAAR applies (introduced in the SDC law by Law 245(I)/2025) ([Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf)).

## 6. Investor / migrant regimes — status as at Sept 2026

### (a) Non-dom — still open; 17-year rule UNCHANGED; paid extension added
- **Test**: an individual **without a Cyprus domicile of origin** is deemed domiciled for SDC once **tax resident in Cyprus for at least 17 of the last 20 years** before the tax year (SDC Law art. 2(3)) — [PwC WWTS – taxes on personal income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/taxes-on-personal-income); [Kleanthous & Platis](https://www.kleanthousplatis.com/news-publications/non-dom-what-it-exempts).
- **Domicile of origin** route: domicile is taken from the Wills and Succession Law; a Cyprus domicile of origin can be displaced by a retained domicile of choice abroad (fact-based) — [Kleanthous & Platis](https://www.kleanthousplatis.com/news-publications/non-dom-what-it-exempts); [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/taxes-on-personal-income).
- **Tax-residency tests**: 183-day rule; **60-day rule** (≥60 days in Cyprus; not resident >183 days in any other single state; business/employment/directorship in a Cyprus tax-resident company not terminated during the year; own or rent a permanent residence in Cyprus). **From 1 Jan 2026 the condition "not tax resident in any other state" was REMOVED** — [PwC WWTS – residence](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/residence); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.cy/globalassets/1.-member-firms/cyprus/shareable-pdf/grant-thornton-cyprus---tax-reform-package-2026.11.pdf); [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf).
- **The 2025 amendment law you saw** = **Law 245(I)/2025, "Ο περί Έκτακτης Εισφοράς για την Άμυνα της Δημοκρατίας (Τροποποιητικός) (Αρ. 4) Νόμος του 2025"**, Gaz. 5070, 31.12.2025 (Tax Department copy exists on mof.gov.cy; content verified via [KPMG's summary of it](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/cyprus/tax-authorities/1809206/circular-22026-extension-of-the-non-dom-regime)). **What it did**: inserted **Article 3D** (alternative SDC regime) and **deleted the rent provisions** from SDC with effect from 1 Jan 2026. It **did NOT extend the 17-year period** and **did NOT bring rents into SDC** (the opposite). Note: SDC Amending (No.3) Law 2025 = **220(I)/2025**, Gaz. 5066, 12.12.2025, dealt only with the reorganisation window (10→11 years) and Art. 8A deadlines (extended to 31.12.2026) — official text [cylaw 2025_1_220.pdf](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2025_1_220.pdf).
- **Article 3D alternative (effective 1 Jan 2026)**: an individual with **no Cyprus domicile of origin** who became **deemed domiciled under the 17/20 rule** may elect to pay a **lump sum of EUR 250,000 for five consecutive tax years (EUR 50,000/year)** instead of SDC on actual dividends/interest; **max two such 5-year periods (10 years)**; application by **30 June** of the first year; **irrevocable**, **non-refundable**, **no foreign tax credit**, no set-off against other taxes — [Kleanthous & Platis](https://www.kleanthousplatis.com/news-publications/non-dom-what-it-exempts); [Andersen Cyprus](https://cy.andersen.com/news/cyprus-introduces-an-alternative-sdc-regime/); [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.cy/globalassets/1.-member-firms/cyprus/shareable-pdf/grant-thornton-cyprus---tax-reform-package-2026.11.pdf).
- **Implementing circular**: Tax Department **Circular 2/2026 issued 29 May 2026**; individuals deemed domiciled in **2024, 2025 or 2026** could apply **by 30 June 2026** (that first window has passed as at 17 Sep 2026) — [KPMG GMS Flash Alert 2026-159, 25 June 2026](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/xx/pdf/2026/06/fa26-159.pdf.coredownload.pdf); [KPMG/mondaq, 30 June 2026](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/cyprus/tax-authorities/1809206/circular-22026-extension-of-the-non-dom-regime) (links the official mof.gov.cy PDF `2026_02.pdf`). Chambers Private Wealth 2026 (Cyprus chapter) still records the regime as available and states "no other current plans or proposals to change the tax regime" — [Chambers Private Wealth 2026, p.199](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Private-Wealth-2026/199/).

### (b) Permanent residence by investment (Regulation 6(2)) and citizenship
- **Regulation 6(2) (fast-track PR)**: minimum **EUR 300,000** qualifying investment, four categories: (i) **new first-sale** house/apartment from a developer (plus VAT); (ii) other qualifying real estate (offices/shops/hotels — resales allowed in this category only); (iii) EUR 300,000 share capital of a Cyprus company with proven physical presence and **≥5 employees in Cyprus**; (iv) EUR 300,000 in units of a qualifying Cyprus **AIF/AIFLNP/RAIF** investing in Cyprus. **Secure annual income: EUR 50,000 principal + EUR 15,000 spouse + EUR 10,000 per minor child** (spouse's income may count); funds must come from abroad (except income may be Cyprus-sourced for categories ii–iv). Processing typically ~6 months; annual confirmations; criminal-record refresh every 3 years — [Papacleovoulou, Regulation 6(2) guide, last reviewed 1 Aug 2026](https://www.papacleovoulou.com/post/cyprus-golden-visa-2026-permanent-residence); [A. Danos & Associates via Mondaq, 17 Sept 2025](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/cyprus/investment-immigration/1679248/navigating-category-62-permanent-residency-in-cyprus-2025-guide).
- **2025/2026 changes**: current policy is the **fourth revision (May 2023)**, read with Migration Department clarifications; the Migration Department's **3 March 2026 transition notice ended** the transitional arrangement that allowed applications under the previous 2021 criteria — every new application is now examined under the criteria in force on its filing date. The **EUR 300,000 / EUR 50,000 thresholds were NOT changed** ([Papacleovoulou, reviewed 1 Aug 2026](https://www.papacleovoulou.com/post/cyprus-golden-visa-2026-permanent-residence)).
- **Citizenship-by-investment: abolished in 2020** — last applications accepted **30 October 2020**; there is **no route to a Cypriot passport by investment today**; the statutory basis was repealed by Parliament in **December 2025** (gk-lawfirm states **12 Dec 2025**; [Xinhua, 5 Dec 2025](http://english.news.cn/20251205/178fce17f1a04fa18b7520f080bdb252/c.html) reports Parliament passed a law removing the Cabinet's power to grant citizenship to foreign investors on the preceding Thursday, i.e. **4 Dec 2025**, and cites **7,329** citizenships granted under the scheme) — [gk-lawfirm, updated 2 Sept 2026](https://gk-lawfirm.com/practice-areas/cyprus-citizenship-by-investment-program/). **Replacement routes**: PR by investment (above), Category F PR for persons living on foreign pension/dividend/interest/rent, **naturalisation after 7 years' legal residence in the preceding 10 plus the final 12 months** (Greek B1 + civics exam; 4–5 years for highly skilled staff of qualifying companies), or 3 years' marriage to a Cypriot (gk-lawfirm, same URL).

## 7. Exit tax on leaving Cyprus

- **Individuals: NO exit tax.** "On exit, Cyprus does not generally impose a personal exit tax on individuals merely because they cease to be their Cyprus tax residence" — [Chambers Private Wealth 2026, Cyprus chapter, p.199](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Private-Wealth-2026/199/). "Individuals: no, Cyprus does not have an exit tax for individuals… unrealized capital gains on their shareholdings or investments are not taxed by Cyprus" — [Cyprus-Consult exit tax guide, 13 April 2026](https://cyprus-consult.com/en/exit-tax-cyprus/).
- **No individual exit tax was introduced by the 2026 reform** — none appears in the reform summaries ([PwC DTU N-1/2026](https://www.pwc.com.cy/en/publications/direct-tax-updates-2025/dtu-n1-2026.pdf); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.cy/globalassets/1.-member-firms/cyprus/shareable-pdf/grant-thornton-cyprus---tax-reform-package-2026.11.pdf); [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf)).
- **What survives departure**: CGT at **20%** on Cyprus-situated immovable property (and on shares in property-rich Cyprus companies — indirect threshold now **20%** from 1 Jan 2026) applies **regardless of the seller's residence** ([PwC WWTS – other taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/other-taxes)). Income from Cyprus sources (e.g. Cyprus rent) remains taxable in the year of departure per the day-count rules.
- **Companies: ATAD exit tax exists** (deemed disposal at market value on transferring assets outside the Cyprus tax net, e.g. to a foreign PE or on relocating tax residence; intra-EU transfers may be spread over 5 years) — [Cyprus-Consult, 13 Apr 2026](https://cyprus-consult.com/en/exit-tax-cyprus/) (source states Law No. 116(I)/2019 of 31 Dec 2019 — see UNVERIFIED for the law number).

## 8. Minimum annual tax / lump-sum floors

- **No minimum income tax** for individuals; liability is purely rate-based on the brackets in §1.
- **GHS floor**: the official rate table sets no minimum amount — 2.65% of actual dividend/interest/rental/other income, capped at EUR 180,000 of income ([gov.cy GESY](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/geniko-systima-ygeias-gesy/)).
- **Self-employed social insurance floor**: minimum insurable earnings by occupational category; the basic insurable-earnings amount set by the Council of Ministers decree published **12 Dec 2025** is **EUR 220.76/week (EUR 11,480/year)** ([PwC N-10-2025, 23 Dec 2025](https://www.pwc.com.cy/en/publications/assets/trs-n10-2025.pdf) — PwC's text labels this "2025" but the decree date and the 2026 announcement it accompanies indicate 2026). At the self-employed rate of 16.6%, the implied minimum annual social-insurance contribution is **≈ EUR 1,905.68** (arithmetic derived, not stated in the source).
- **SDC floor**: none — SDC is proportionate to income.
- **Non-dom regime: no minimum annual tax.** A resident non-dom pays **nil SDC** on dividends/interest (and rents are outside SDC for everyone from 2026) and **no income tax on those items**; GHS **2.65%** still applies. The **EUR 50,000/year (EUR 250,000 per 5-year block)** charge is **not a standing minimum** — it applies only if the individual, after completing the 17-year deemed-domicile period, **elects** the Article 3D alternative ([Kleanthous & Platis](https://www.kleanthousplatis.com/news-publications/non-dom-what-it-exempts); [KPMG Flash Alert 2026-159](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/xx/pdf/2026/06/fa26-159.pdf.coredownload.pdf)).
- Other floors/flat charges: fund-industry carried-interest mode **8% with a minimum liability of EUR 10,000/year** ([Chambers Private Wealth 2026, p.198](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Private-Wealth-2026/198/)).

## 9. Reform package — measures, exact dates, and 2027+ outlook

**Timeline**: announced/approved by Cabinet **29 Oct 2025** → bills **Gaz. 4362, 31 Oct 2025** → **Parliament vote 22 Dec 2025** → laws **Gaz. 5070, 31 Dec 2025** → **effective 1 Jan 2026** (unless stated otherwise). Sources: [CNA 30/10/2025](https://aman-alliance.org/Home/ContentDetail/96554); [mof.gov.cy gazette 4362](https://www.mof.gov.cy/mof/gpo/gazette.nsf/1A8295B64ECDB525C2258D340038546B/$file/4362%2031%2010%202025%20PARARTHMA%20EKTO.pdf); [PwC DTU N-1/2026](https://www.pwc.com.cy/en/publications/direct-tax-updates-2025/dtu-n1-2026.pdf); [Kendris 9/1/2026](https://www.kendris.com/de/news-insights/2026/01/09/cyprus-tax-reform-2026/).

**Personal / SDC measures (1 Jan 2026):** PIT brackets to 0%/20%/25%/30%/35% with 0% band to EUR 22,000 and 35% above EUR 72,000; child/housing/green/natural-disaster deductions; ex-gratia 20% over EUR 200,000; foreign pension 5% over EUR 5,000; **SDC dividends 17%→5%** (17% transitional for pre-2026 profits to 2031); **SDC on rents abolished**; **DDD abolished** (2024–2025 profits transitional, crystallising 31/12/2026 and 31/12/2027); disguised dividends 10%; **non-dom extension via EUR 250,000/5 years (Art. 3D)**; 60-day rule relaxed; mandatory returns for residents aged 25–71; audited-accounts threshold EUR 70,000→120,000; CGT property-rich threshold 50%→20% and higher lifetime exemptions. Sources: [PwC DTU N-1/2026](https://www.pwc.com.cy/en/publications/direct-tax-updates-2025/dtu-n1-2026.pdf); [Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf); [Andersen](https://cy.andersen.com/news/cyprus-news-cyprus-tax-reform-comprehensive-update-effective-from-1-january-2026/); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.cy/globalassets/1.-member-firms/cyprus/shareable-pdf/grant-thornton-cyprus---tax-reform-package-2026.11.pdf).

**Corporate measures (1 Jan 2026):** CIT **12.5% → 15%**; loss carry-forward **5 → 7 years**; R&D 120% super-deduction to 2030; entertainment cap EUR 17,086 → **30,000**; crypto-asset gains **8%**; share options **8%** (cap EUR 1m/10 years, per Andersen); 5% WHT on dividends to low-tax jurisdictions; **stamp duty repealed** for documents signed after 1 Jan 2026 (Law 239(I)/2025). Sources: [Andersen](https://cy.andersen.com/news/cyprus-news-cyprus-tax-reform-comprehensive-update-effective-from-1-january-2026/); [Kendris](https://www.kendris.com/de/news-insights/2026/01/09/cyprus-tax-reform-2026/); [PwC DTU N-1/2026](https://www.pwc.com.cy/en/publications/direct-tax-updates-2025/dtu-n1-2026.pdf).

**2027:** Cyprus–**Curaçao** DTT effective **1 Jan 2027** ([PwC significant developments](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/significant-developments)); Cyprus–Oman DTT effective 1 Jan 2026 (same URL); DDD for **2025 profits** crystallises **31/12/2027** at 17%; dividends out of 2025 profits paid during **2026 and 2027** between Cyprus resident companies at 17% ([Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf)). No further personal-tax measures for 2027 were found; Chambers (2026 edition) states there are "no other current plans or proposals to change the tax regime" ([Chambers Private Wealth 2026, p.199](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Private-Wealth-2026/199/)).

**Later-dated measures:** from **1 Jan 2031**, redemption of units/shares in CIS in corporate form is treated as a **dividend** ([PwC income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cyprus/individual/income-determination); [Kendris](https://www.kendris.com/de/news-insights/2026/01/09/cyprus-tax-reform-2026/)); all 17% SDC transitional rules expire by **31/12/2031** ([Treppides](https://www.treppides.com/images/pdf-newsletters/January2026/Cyprus_Tax_Reform_-_A_simplified_guide.pdf)).

**Non-dom 17-year clock:** **not changed** — still 17 of the last 20 years for deemed domicile; the reform added the *paid* extension (2 × 5 years at EUR 250,000 each) rather than extending the clock.
