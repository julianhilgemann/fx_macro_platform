# Exit Tax / Deemed-Disposal-on-Emigration Extract — 10 Jurisdictions
**Research date: 17 September 2026.** Figures labelled 2025 or 2026 throughout. "No exit tax" claims are supported by a cited absence-source. Where sources disagree, both are given.

## ⚠️ Three corrections to the brief's premises (verified, not assumed)

1. **Greece has NO personal exit tax, and art. 5A is not an exit tax.** Art. 5A of Law 4172/2013 is the **inbound** €100,000 lump-sum regime for new residents. The €500,000 in art. 5A is an **investment requirement**, not an exit threshold. Greece's ATAD exit tax (art. 66A ITC) applies **only to corporate income taxpayers** — "Individuals are not included in the scope of the application of those rules… Greece did not opt for such an extension" ([IBFD Intertax 2022, pp. 626/634](http://library.ibfd.org/custom/web/SD_PDF/scans/2022/G-I/INTERTAX/8-9_619-634.pdf)). The **44%** figure is Greece's top **PIT** marginal rate on employment/pension income, not an exit-tax rate ([KPMG Flash Alert 2025-277](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-277.html)).
2. **Poland DOES have a personal exit tax** — art. 30da of the PIT Act, **PLN 4,000,000** threshold, 19% (or 3%). It is in force and return-filing is required; only its **collection** is repeatedly deferred ([ETL Global, July 2026](https://www.etl-global.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Global-Mobility-Exit-Tax-2026-July.pdf)).
3. **Estonia's income/corporate rate is 22% in both 2025 and 2026 — it did NOT rise to 24%.** EMTA's own 2026 rate page and KPMG's Tax Card 2026 both state 22% ([EMTA tax rates](https://emta.ee/en/private-client/taxes-and-payment/declaration-income/tax-rates); [KPMG Tax Card 2026](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/ee/pdf/tax-cards/2026%20Tax%20Card-web.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). The 24% figure is **UNVERIFIED** — no source found supporting it. (2024 was 20%; 2025 was 22%.)

## Per-jurisdiction notes

### Singapore — no exit tax (VERIFIED)
- **No CGT, no exit tax.** Tax residency is status-based: citizen/PR normally residing in SG; or a foreigner staying **≥183 days** in the prior calendar year; or **3 consecutive years**; or a continuous period **straddling 2 calendar years** totalling **≥183 days**; a work pass valid **≥1 year** also confers resident treatment, reviewed at tax clearance ([IRAS, Working out my tax residency](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-residency-and-tax-rates/working-out-my-tax-residency)). Resident PIT is progressive **0%–24%**.
- On emigration the only requirement is **tax clearance (Form IR21)** for departing foreign employees — a collection mechanism, not a departure tax ([IRAS, Tax Clearance for Employees](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employers/tax-clearance-for-foreign-spr-employees-(ir21)/tax-clearance-for-employees)).
- **2025/2026 change — Refundable Investment Credit (RITC):** Income Tax (Refundable Investment Credits) Regulations 2025, **SL 577/2025, in force 1 Sep 2025**, amended by **S 147/2026 (1 Apr 2026)**. The rate is **10%, 30% or 50%** of qualifying expenditure (capex on plant/property/equipment; manpower costs; etc.) across prescribed activities including energy-efficiency/decarbonisation ([Singapore Statutes Online](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/ITA1947-S577-2025)). **No 2025/2026 change to the 183-day or 3-year residency tests was found.** GIP status for 2025/2026: **UNVERIFIED** (no official source retrieved).

### UAE — no exit tax (VERIFIED)
- **No personal income tax and no exit tax.** Corporate tax is **9%**, effective **1 June 2023** (Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022).
- **DMTT: 15%, effective 1 January 2025.** Cabinet Decision No. 142 of 2024 on the Imposition of Top-up Tax on MNEs, **issued 31 December 2024, effective from 1 January 2025**; the annexure defines "**Minimum Rate**" as "**fifteen percent (15%)**"; it applies to fiscal years beginning on or after 1 Jan 2025 ([UAE MoF primary text](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/English-Cabinet-Decision-142-of-2024-on-Top-up-Tax-on-MNEs-1.pdf); [FTA copy](https://tax.gov.ae//Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Cabinet-Decision-No-142-of-2024-on-Top-up-Tax-on-MNEs.pdf)).
- **2025/2026 Golden Visa/residency changes:** reported expansions of eligibility categories and property thresholds. Exact 2026 thresholds and durations: **UNVERIFIED** (secondary immigration-consultancy sources only; no official ICP/MoF page retrieved).
- UAE tax-residency day-count rules (183-day / 90-day): **UNVERIFIED** in this research.

### New Zealand — no exit tax (VERIFIED)
- **No general CGT and no exit tax on individuals emigrating.** Departure does not trigger deemed disposal of shares or property.
- **Transitional resident exemption = 48 months (4 years)**, from the **first day** of NZ tax residence (permanent place of abode or 183-day rule). IRD states the period ends at "**the end of the 48th month after the month in which they acquired a permanent place of abode in New Zealand or satisfied the 183-day rule**", and notes it can run longer than 48 months ([IRD Tax Information Bulletin Vol 37 No 6, July 2025, paras 160–163](https://www.taxtechnical.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/tt/pdfs/tib/volume-37---2025/tib-vol37-no6.pdf)). During it, almost all foreign-sourced income is exempt (s CW 27) except employment income and services income; CFC/FIF/financial-arrangements/trust rules apply as if the person were non-resident (s HR 8(1)) ([IRD, Temporary tax exemption](https://www.ird.govt.nz/roles/new-zealand-tax-residents/temporary-tax-exemption)).
- **Rules that DO bite in cross-border cases (not exit taxes):** the **FIF** rules ([Income Tax Act 2007, cases of entry into and exit from FIF rules](https://legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/0097/latest/DLM1515707.html)); the **financial arrangements** rules, where ceasing NZ residence requires a **base price adjustment** — with an exception for cash-basis persons ceasing residence before the first day of the fourth income year after first becoming resident (s EW 30(1)) (TIB Vol 37 No 6, paras 178–184); and **trust** rules, where foreign-sourced trustee income becomes assessable once a settlor is resident and not transitional (s HC 25/HC 26).
- **Trustee tax rate 39%** (up from 33%) for the **2024–25 and later income years**, with 33% retained where trustee income ≤ **NZ$10,000** ([IRD Tax Policy, Special Report: 39% Trustee Tax Rate, 9 Apr 2024](https://www.taxpolicy.ird.govt.nz/publications/2024/2024-sr-39-trustee-tax-rate)). **No 2026 change identified.**

### Cyprus — no exit tax (VERIFIED)
- **No exit tax** on individuals ceasing Cyprus tax residence.
- **Residency:** **183-day** rule, or the **60-day rule** — at least **60 days** in Cyprus, not more than **183 days** in any other single state, permanent home in Cyprus, and a qualifying Cyprus business/employment/office connection not terminated during the year ([60-day vs 183-day guide, reviewed 21 Aug 2026](https://www.papacleovoulou.com/post/cyprus-60-day-vs-183-day-rule-remote-workers)).
- **Non-dom: retained.** The **17-year** non-dom rule (exemption from Special Defence Contribution on dividends/interest for 17 years after becoming Cyprus tax resident, subject to the domicile tests) survives the reform — the reform "**preserves key competitiveness features**", explicitly keeping "the non-dom regime and the 50% exemption on employment income" ([WTS Cyprus, Tax Reform: Key Changes](https://www.wts-advisory.com/wts.com/publishing-articles/2026/Cyprus%20Tax%20Reform.pdf)).
- **2026 Cyprus tax reform — approved 22 December 2025, majority effective 1 January 2026** (WTS, above):
  - Corporate income tax **12.5% → 15%**.
  - **SDC on dividends 17% → 5%** for Cyprus tax-resident and domiciled individuals; **deemed dividend distribution abolished**; **3% SDC on rental income abolished**.
  - Tax losses: 5 years → **7 years**. R&D super-deduction extended to **2025–2030**. Stamp duty and insurance premium tax abolished.
  - Personal income tax bands expanded and a tax-free threshold introduced (band details: **UNVERIFIED** — the WTS table's numeric cells did not extract).

### Malta — no exit tax (VERIFIED)
- **No exit tax.**
- **Remittance basis:** foreign-source income is taxed at a flat **15%** only when **received in Malta**; unremitted foreign income is not taxed. Maltese-source income is taxed at **35%** ([FinanceMalta / WH Partners](https://financemalta.org/industry-updates/malta-s-new-individual-tax-programme-rules-key-changes-ahead-of-2027-launch)).
- **Current programmes and minimum annual tax (until 31 Dec 2026):** GRP and TRP **€15,000**; MRP **€7,500** (plus **€500** per dependent); UNPP **€10,000** (plus **€5,000** where both spouses receive a UN pension). Application fees **€2,500–€6,000** (FinanceMalta, above).
- **New Individual Tax Programme Rules, 2026 — Legal Notice 195 of 2026, in force 1 January 2027** (made under arts. 56(23) and 96, Income Tax Act Cap. 123) ([legislation.mt](https://legislation.mt/eli/ln/2026/195/eng); [Attard Baldacchino ITP guide, updated July 2026](https://www.attardbaldacchino.com/itp-malta-individual-tax-programme)):
  - Four statuses: **global resident**; **EU/EEA/Swiss resident**; **retired pensioner**; **UN pensioner**.
  - Rate **15%** on foreign income received in Malta (beneficiary, spouse, defined children); non-qualifying income **35%**.
  - **Minimum annual tax: €35,000** (global / EU-EEA-Swiss), **€15,000** (retired pensioner), **€20,000** (UN pensioner; UN pension itself exempt). Payable in full in both the grant year and the cessation year.
  - **Qualifying property: purchase ≥ €700,000 or lease ≥ €14,000 per year**, Malta or Gozo.
  - **Administrative fee €8,500** non-refundable on application; term **5 years**, renewable in 5-year periods for **€2,500**.
  - **Transitional:** statuses granted, and applications received, up to **31 December 2026** continue to apply **until 31 December 2031**.

### Greece — no individual exit tax; corporate exit tax exists (VERIFIED)
- **Individuals are OUTSIDE the exit-tax rules.** "**Individuals are not included in the scope of the application of those rules**, however, domestic legislation could have arguably expanded the rules of Article 5 ATAD to also do so… However, **Greece did not opt for such an extension**." Base-erosion concerns on individuals' asset/residence transfers can therefore be addressed "**only through a GAAR**" (art. 38 TPC) ([IBFD Intertax 2022, 8-9, pp. 619–634, quote at p. 634](http://library.ibfd.org/custom/web/SD_PDF/scans/2022/G-I/INTERTAX/8-9_619-634.pdf)).
- **Corporate exit tax — art. 66A ITC (Law 4172/2013):** applies to corporate income taxpayers; tax base is the **difference between market value and tax value** of transferred assets; computed at the generally applicable **corporate income tax rate**; declaration must be filed **at least 3 days before** the triggering event, tax paid **within 3 days** of filing unless the taxpayer is entitled to **defer and pay in instalments under art. 66A(4)** (which duplicates ATAD art. 5(2)–(3)); declaration form per Governor's Decision **A.1231/2020** (IBFD, above).
- **Art. 5A — inbound lump-sum (this is the provision the brief mislabels as the exit tax).** Verified against the consolidated statute ([art. 5A, Law 4172/2013, taxheaven consolidated text](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/4172/2013)):
  - **€100,000** flat annual tax on **all** foreign-source income, regardless of amount; **+€20,000** per relative.
  - Eligibility: not a Greek tax resident for the previous **7 of 8 years**; **and** invest **≥ €500,000** in Greek real estate/businesses/securities/shares, completed **within 3 years** of application (investment requirement waived for holders of an art. 16 L.4251/2014 investment residence permit).
  - Duration **15 fiscal years**, **no extension possible**; paid **in one instalment by the last business day of December**; not creditable against other Greek tax liabilities; foreign tax not creditable. Inheritance/gift-tax exemption for foreign movable property.
  - Cessation: failure to pay the full flat amount, or failure to complete the investment within 3 years, ends the regime, and total Greek tax on foreign income for those years **cannot be less than €100,000 per year**.
- **Law 5222/2025 (published in the Government Gazette 28 July 2025)** amended arts. 5A, 5B and 5C: relatives may be added only for remaining years of the 15-year window; the 15-year duration is fixed with **no extensions**; foreign movable property exempted from inheritance/gift tax in two cases; and **art. 5C's 50% exemption is no longer limited to newly created positions**, with a reciprocity condition for exchange of information ([Regfollower, 4 Aug 2025](https://regfollower.com/greece-expands-and-strengthens-rules-for-alternative-tax-regimes/); [Orbitax, 5 Aug 2025](https://orbitax.com/news/country/article/Greece-Tightens-Rules-and-Broa-59552)).
- **2026 PIT scale — Law 5246/2025, adopted 7 November 2025, effective tax year 2026:** 9% (0–10,000); 20% (10,000.01–20,000); 26% (20,000.01–30,000); 34% (30,000.01–40,000); **39% (40,000.01–60,000)**; **44% (>60,000)**. Real-estate income: 15% / 25% / 35% / 45% above €36,000. Electronic-payment incentives extended through 2026 ([KPMG Flash Alert 2025-277, 16 Dec 2025](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-277.html); [Bernitsas, Nov 2025](https://bernitsaslaw.com/2025/11/18/tax-alert-new-law-52462025)).
- **A further Greek tax bill was submitted to public consultation in June 2026** amending the ITC ([Bernitsas Tax Alert No. 150, June 2026](https://bernitsaslaw.com/sites/default/files/2026-06/June%202026%20Tax%20Alert.pdf)) — content of ITC amendments **UNVERIFIED** (PDF text extraction failed).

### Estonia — no individual exit tax; corporate exit tax (VERIFIED)
- **Corporate exit tax — § 54⁵ TuMS ("Tulumaks lahkumisel").** Charged on the difference between **market value and carrying amount** of assets when a **resident company** moves assets to a PE in another EU Member State or a third country. Excluded: assets taken out for financing securities, as collateral, or to meet prudential capital requirements/liquidity management, **if they revert to Estonia within 12 months**. Instalments over **up to 5 years** where the resident company takes assets to a PE in a Contracting State or becomes resident of another Contracting State (conditions in § 54⁵(4)–(7)). Declared in **Annex 7 of form TSD, code 7014**; return and payment by the **10th day of the month following the taxable period** ([EMTA, § 54⁵ Exit income tax](https://emta.ee/en/business-client/taxes-and-payment/income-and-social-taxes/specifications-upon-taxation-income-tax/ss-545-exit-income-tax-income-tax-act); Estonian original: [Eestist väljaviidud vara](https://emta.ee/node/330/chapter/32687/pdf)).
- **Individuals are OUTSIDE § 54⁵.** Every formulation of the charge is limited to a "**residendist äriühing**" (resident company) — moving assets to a PE, or becoming resident of another Contracting State. The EMTA individual "Moving away from Estonia" page imposes **no departure tax or deemed disposal** ([EMTA, Moving away from Estonia](https://www.emta.ee/en/private-client/consignments-travel-moving/moving/moving-away-estonia)). Estonia taxes company profits on **distribution**, so emigration of an individual does not trigger a shareholder-level charge.
- **Rates — 22% in both 2025 and 2026, NOT 24%.** EMTA: "**2026** — The rate of withheld income tax is **22%**"; "**2025** — The rate of withheld income tax is **22%**" ([EMTA tax rates](https://emta.ee/en/private-client/taxes-and-payment/declaration-income/tax-rates)). KPMG Tax Card 2026: "In 2026, the standard tax rate is **22%** (calculated as **22/78** of the net distribution)", and the same card applies 22% to the exit charge on assets transferred out ([KPMG Estonia Tax Card 2026](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/ee/pdf/tax-cards/2026%20Tax%20Card-web.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). 2024 was **20%**; the step to 22% took effect in 2025. **A 24% rate for 2026 is UNVERIFIED.**
- The **Security Tax Act** (julgeolekumaks) changes were passed by the Riigikogu ([Estonian Ministry of Finance](https://www.fin.ee/uudised/riigikogu-kiitis-heaks-julgeolekumaksu-muudatused); [OECD, Assessing Estonia's Security Tax Act, Feb 2026](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/els-case-studies_a664bf68-en/assessing-estonia-s-security-tax-act_e1d35784-en.html)). **The precise 2026 headline rates attributable to it are UNVERIFIED** — the only rate evidence obtained (EMTA, KPMG) shows 22% for 2026.

### Czechia — no individual exit tax; corporate-only transfer rule (VERIFIED)
- **No personal exit tax for individuals.** The Czech transfer-of-assets-out rule applies to "**Only legal entities; in the case of transfer of assets abroad without change of ownership**", taxed at the **corporate income tax rate = 21%**, on "the usual/market price of the property (fixed assets, current assets) reduced by its tax residual value"; return due **4 months** after the tax period (electronic) or **6 months** (audit/tax adviser); some carve-outs if assets return within **12 months** ([ETL Global, Exit Tax Rules in Europe, July 2026](https://www.etl-global.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Global-Mobility-Exit-Tax-2026-July.pdf)). Individual emigration triggers no deemed disposal.
- **2026 individual figures (VERIFIED):** the **23%** rate applies to income exceeding **36× the average wage** = **CZK 1,762,812** annually (**CZK 146,901** monthly) for 2026; income below is taxed at **15%**. Average wage for 2026 **CZK 48,967**; maximum social-security assessment base **CZK 2,350,416**; annual non-cash benefit exemption limit **CZK 24,483.50** ([ASB Group, Legislative changes in payroll for 2026](https://asbgroup.eu/en/news/legislative-changes-in-payroll-for-2026/)).
- **Flat tax (paušální daň) 2026:** Band 1 **CZK 9,162/month** (CZK 109,944 for the year; reduced retroactively to January by law 90/2026 Coll. from an initially paid CZK 9,984); Band 2 **CZK 16,745**; Band 3 **CZK 27,139** ([Taxorio, 2026 bands](https://www.taxorio.cz/en/blog/flat-tax-pausalni-dan-czech-republic-foreigners-2026)). **The 2025 flat-tax amounts and the 2025 23% threshold (CZK) are UNVERIFIED** in this research.
- The **2024 consolidation package** introduced the 23% band (and the 0.6% employee sickness contribution); the 3×-average-wage design is the statutory mechanic. Precise 2024 legislative citation: **UNVERIFIED**.

### Poland — YES, personal exit tax exists (art. 30da PIT Act) (VERIFIED)
- **Poland DOES impose an exit tax on individuals** — contrary to the brief. ETL Global: "Taxpayers: **The individuals** who transfer assets outside of Poland or change their tax residency, resulting in Poland losing the right to tax the income from those assets" ([ETL Global, July 2026](https://www.etl-global.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Global-Mobility-Exit-Tax-2026-July.pdf)).
  - **Threshold: aggregate market value of transferred assets below PLN 4,000,000 → exit tax does not apply.**
  - **Base:** sum of unrealised gains — "the difference between the market value and the tax value of the asset", valued as at **the day before the change in tax residency**.
  - **Rate: 19%** (standard), or **3%** where the tax value cannot be determined. Forms **PIT-NZ** (standard) or **PIT-NZS**.
  - **Additional gate for non-business assets:** exit tax applies to shares, stocks, securities, financial instruments and fund participation titles **only if the taxpayer has had Polish tax residency for at least 5 years in the last 10 years**.
- **Deferral — this is the key practical point.** Although the return must be filed and tax is nominally due by the 7th day of the month following the month the PLN 4m threshold is exceeded, the Minister of Finance has repeatedly deferred **collection** by regulation (earlier: 27 June 2019, Dz.U. 2019 poz. 1204; 21 Dec 2021, Dz.U. 2021 poz. 2409; 2 Aug 2023, Dz.U. 2023 poz. 1611). A further deferral moved payment to **the 7th day of the month after the asset is lost (gain realised) before 1 December 2027, and to 31 December 2027 otherwise** ([Gazeta Prawna, 18 Aug 2025](https://www.gazetaprawna.pl/podatki/artykuly/10784175,exit-tax-termin-znow-przedluzony-ale-na-reforme-jeszcze-poczekamy.html)); ETL confirms deadlines "**have generally been extended until the end of 2025**" and, per the 2026 edition, beyond ([ETL Global, July 2026](https://www.etl-global.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Global-Mobility-Exit-Tax-2026-July.pdf)). **Whether a regulation finally fixing end-2027 was signed is UNVERIFIED** (the Aug-2025 source describes a draft in public consultation).
- **Litigation:** the Warsaw Voivodeship Administrative Court referred three questions to the **CJEU on 29 May 2025 (III SA/Wa 566/25)** — whether Poland may demand exit tax at the moment of change of residence (or with instalments) rather than on disposal, and whether tax on hypothetical value breaches EU free movement (arts. 21 and 45 TFEU). The Ministry justifies each deferral by reference to the CJEU's **C-581/17 (26 Feb 2019)** ruling that immediate exit-tax payment breaches EU law (Gazeta Prawna, above).
- **Polish PIT scale, 2026:** unchanged since 2022 — **12%** up to **PLN 120,000** (less the **PLN 3,600** tax-reducing amount), then **PLN 10,800 + 32%** of the excess over PLN 120,000 ([Infor, PIT w 2026 roku](https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/podatki/pit/pit/rozliczenia/7529565,pit-w-2026-roku-progi-podatkowe-kwota-wolna-od-podatku-skala-podatkowa-czy-zajdzie-zmiana-z-powodu-kwoty-minimum-egzystencji.html)). The same 120,000 threshold applied in 2025. A rise in the second threshold has been discussed and then dropped — **not legislated for 2026**.
- **Lump-sum for new residents:** **PLN 200,000 per year** on foreign income, for a maximum of **10 consecutive tax years**, plus **PLN 100,000 per year** for a spouse/child added to the regime; conditions include transferring residence to Poland, **not having resided in Poland for at least 5 of the 6 years** preceding relocation, and filing a one-time declaration ([Sadkowski, 5 Mar 2026](https://sadkowskiiwspolnicy.pl/en/lump-sum-for-tax-residence-relocation-to-poland-as-an-attractive-instrument-for-international-tax-planning/); [Rödl & Partner](https://www.roedl.com/en/insights/tax-reliefs-individuals-moving-tax-residence-poland/)). The exact statutory article (commonly cited as art. 21(1)(147) PIT Act): **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Polish Holding Company (PST) regime and 2026 changes:** **UNVERIFIED** — no primary/authoritative source retrieved in this research.

### United Kingdom — NO general exit tax (VERIFIED)
- **"There is no general exit tax for individuals resident in the UK, when they cease residence, currently."** Tax charges can nonetheless be triggered on ceasing UK residence where, e.g., gains were rolled over into a tax-efficient investment held at departure (the rolled-over gain becomes chargeable), where a donor's gift was rolled over, where a self-employed individual is deemed to cease trading, or where a trust ceases to be UK tax resident ([ETL Global, July 2026](https://www.etl-global.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Global-Mobility-Exit-Tax-2026-July.pdf)). ETL classes the UK with France and Luxembourg as jurisdictions where "**only very general regulations exist for tax liabilities upon a cease of residency**".
- **Temporary non-residence (TNR) — capital gains, TCGA 1992 s.10A (rewritten for disposals from 6 April 2019).** An individual is "**temporarily non-resident**" if they: had **sole UK residence** for a residence period; then one or more periods without sole UK residence; were resident for **at least 4 of the 7 tax years** immediately preceding the year of departure; **and the period of temporary non-residence is 5 years or less**. Gains accruing in the intervening period are treated as accruing in the year of return ([HMRC CG26540](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg26540); [HMRC CG26500](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg26500)).
  - **Exclusions:** gains on assets acquired during the absence are generally outside s.10A — but **not** assets held in a non-resident trust or a closely controlled non-resident company; and anti-avoidance provisions catch rolled-over/deferred gains (CG26510).
  - **Pre-2013-14 departures** were governed by the older s.10A test requiring UK residence in **4 of the 7** years and non-residence for **less than 5 full tax years**, with ESC D2 relief ([HMRC CG26110](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg26110)).
- **TNR for income:** **ITEPA 2003 s.10A** (employment income) and **ITTOIA 2005 s.832A** (trade, profession, vocation and other income), applying the same 5-year framework ([ITTOIA 2005 s.832A, legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/cy/ukpga/2005/5/section/832A/data.pdf)).
- **The 5-year rule and UK property:** disposals of UK land by non-residents fall within **Non-Resident Capital Gains Tax (NRCGT)** regardless of the TNR rule, with **60-day** reporting and payment via a CGT on UK Property Account ([HMRC HS307](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/non-resident-capital-gains-for-land-and-property-in-the-uk-self-assessment-helpsheet-hs307/hs307-non-resident-capital-gains-on-direct-and-indirect-disposals-of-interest-in-uk-land-and-property-2025); [ICAEW, 60-day reporting reminder, Sep 2025](https://www.icaew.com/insights/tax-news/2025/sep-2025/taxpayers-reminded-of-cgt-60-day-reporting-requirement)). HMRC's TNR helpsheet for 2025 is **HS278** ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/temporary-non-residents-and-capital-gains-tax-hs278-self-assessment-helpsheet/hs278-temporary-non-residents-and-capital-gains-tax-2025)).
- **2025/2026 changes to TNR:** **none identified.** **No source was found showing any Autumn Budget 2025 change to the 5-year TNR rule.** The OTS had previously recommended abolishing or shortening it; **no legislation to that effect was identified — treat proposals as not enacted.**
