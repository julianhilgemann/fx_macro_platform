## 2. Capital gains on LISTED securities — long-term resident individual

**Year: 2025 (same rules in 2024 and 2026).**

- **Headline: 28% flat autonomous rate** on the positive balance of gains/losses from disposals of `partes sociais` and other `valores mobiliários` — **CIRS art. 72.º n.º 1 al. c)** (cross-referring art. 10.º n.º 1 al. b)). Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs72.aspx
- **There IS a holding-period relief — but it is a partial exclusion, not an exemption. CIRS art. 43.º n.º 5, added by Lei n.º 31/2024, de 28 de junho (in force 29 June 2024)**, applies to *valores mobiliários admitidos à negociação* and to units in **open-ended** collective investment undertakings:
  - held **> 2 years and < 5 years → 10% of the gain excluded** ⇒ effective **25.2%**
  - held **≥ 5 years and < 8 years → 20% excluded** ⇒ effective **22.4%**
  - held **≥ 8 years → 30% excluded** ⇒ effective **19.6%**
  - **≤ 2 years → no exclusion, full 28%**
  Sources: verbatim statutory text, Lei n.º 31/2024: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/Lei_31_2024.pdf ; consolidated art. 43.º: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs43.aspx ; effective rates 25.2/22.4/19.6%: https://eco.sapo.pt/descodificador/como-declarar-e-poupar-em-irs-os-seus-investimentos-financeiros/
- **There is NO 365-day exemption for listed securities.** The 365-day rule is (a) the **trigger for mandatory aggregation** (below) and (b) a genuine **full exemption for crypto-assets only** (art. 10.º n.º 22 CIRS: gains/losses on crypto held ≥ 365 days excluded). Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs10.aspx
- **2023+ mandatory aggregation for short holdings — CIRS art. 72.º n.º 14** (added by Lei n.º 12/2022, applicable to income from 1 Jan 2023 per art. 280.º n.º 7 of that law): the balance of gains/losses on **assets held < 365 days** is **mandatorily aggregated** (`englobamento obrigatório`) when the taxpayer's taxable income **including that balance** is **equal to or above the entry threshold of the last bracket of art. 68.º n.º 1** — i.e. **€83,696 for income year 2025** and **€86,634 for income year 2026** → taxed at progressive rates up to 48% plus solidarity up to 5%. Source (art. 72.º n.º 14 text): https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs72.aspx
- **35% rate** applies instead of 28% where the issuer is domiciled in a blacklisted jurisdiction (art. 72.º n.º 18 al. b)). Same URL.
- **Live controversy (dated 6 Oct 2025):** Abreu Advogados reports that the AT applied the art. 43.º n.º 5 exclusion only to disposals reported in the "domestic" annex (Annex G) in the 2025 filing season, denying it to foreign-source securities (Annex J), and argues this is contrary to the issuer-neutral statute and to Art. 63 TFEU. https://abreuadvogados.com/en/conhecimento/publications/ifa2025/long-term-capital-gains-relief-in-portugal-statutory-neutrality-vs-administrative-restriction/ — note the eco.sapo.pt guide (contra) states the holding period counts for foreign funds declared in Annex J. **Disputed; flag to any client.**

## 3. Dividend tax — resident individual

**Year: 2025/2026.** Headline **28% withholding at source, liberatory/final** — CIRS art. 71.º n.º 1. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs71.aspx
Optional aggregation (`englobamento`): dividends are then included at **only 50% of gross** for Portuguese / EU / EEA distributing companies — **CIRS art. 40.º-A n.ºs 1, 2 and 4**. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs40a.aspx
**Effective/combined:** 28% flat if not aggregated. If aggregated, 50% inclusion taxed at progressive rates → theoretical maximum effective burden at the 53% top marginal = **26.5% of the gross dividend** (derived: 50% × (48% + 5%)); below the top brackets aggregation is usually worse than 28%. Mandatory aggregation does **not** apply to dividends (art. 72.º n.º 14 covers only art. 10.º n.º 1 al. b) securities gains held <365 days).

## 4. Net wealth tax + inheritance/gift tax

**Net wealth tax: NO general net wealth tax in Portugal.** PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries: "There are no net wealth/worth taxes in Portugal." https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/portugal/individual/other-taxes

**Property-based annual additional tax = AIMI (Adicional ao IMI), CIMI arts. 135.º-A to 135.º-L. Year: 2025 (brackets fixed in the CIMI, not indexed):**
- Threshold: **€600,000 deducted from the aggregate VPT (rateable value) of the taxpayer's urban property** — CIMI art. 135.º-C n.º 2 al. a). https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cimi/Pages/cimi135c.aspx
- Rates (individuals): **0.7%** on the base up to €1,000,000; **marginal 1%** on €1,000,000–€2,000,000; **marginal 1.5%** above €2,000,000; **doubled thresholds if the joint option under art. 135.º-D n.º 1 is exercised (married/cohabiting)** — CIMI art. 135.º-F n.ºs 1–3. Legal persons **0.4%**; `heranças indivisas` 0.7%; property of entities under a privileged tax regime **7.5%** (n.º 5 — expressly not applicable to individuals, n.º 6). https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cimi/Pages/cimi135f.aspx
- No 2025/2026 change to the AIMI rate structure found in OE2026 (Lei n.º 73-A/2025).
- AIMI low-income exemption threshold (widely quoted as €153,300): **UNVERIFIED** — art. 135.º-E page did not return text.

**Inheritance and gifts (Imposto do Selo), year 2025/2026:**
- **0% (exemption)** for gratuitous transfers to **spouse / `unido de facto`, descendants and ascendants** — Código do Imposto do Selo **art. 6.º al. e)**: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/selo/Pages/selo6.aspx
- **10%** for gratuitous transfers to **all other beneficiaries** (inheritance and gifts of movable and immovable property) — **Verba 1.2 da Tabela Geral do Imposto do Selo**. Where the transfer is a **gift** (`doação`), a further **0.8%** applies (Verba 1.1). Source (AT FAQ): https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/questoes_frequentes/Pages/faqs-00031.aspx ; corroborated by PwC: "Free acquisition of goods by individuals (inheritance and gifts) is taxed under the stamp tax at 10%. Donation of property is subject to an additional rate of 0.8%." https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/portugal/individual/other-taxes

## 5. Pre-existing foreign capital of a NEW resident — worldwide taxation from day one

**Yes — worldwide taxation from day one; no remittance basis, no territorial element, no entry step-up.**
- **CIRS art. 15.º n.º 1:** "Sendo as pessoas residentes em território português, o IRS incide sobre a **totalidade dos seus rendimentos, incluindo os obtidos fora desse território**." Non-residents are taxed only on Portuguese-source income (n.º 2). https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs15.aspx
- **Residence test — CIRS art. 16.º n.º 1:** more than **183 days** (consecutive or not) in any 12-month period beginning or ending in the year, **or** having available habitual residence implying current intention to keep and occupy it. Days count if any part of the day includes an overnight stay (n.º 2). Art. 16.º n.º 3/4 provides partial-year residence rules. https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs16.aspx
- Consequence: pre-existing foreign holdings are not taxed on arrival, but **all income and gains arising after residence begins are in scope**, and the acquisition value remains the historic cost (no step-up on immigration). Relief only via DTT credit/exemption (art. 81.º) or a special regime (IFICI / art. 12.º-A — see §6).

## 6. Special regimes available as of 2025 / 2026

### 6a. IFICI — "NHR 2.0" (EBF art. 58.º-A, added by Lei n.º 82/2023, art. 263)
- **Headline rate: 20% flat** on **net category A and B income** earned in the eligible activities, for **10 consecutive years from the year of registration as Portuguese resident**; may be resumed for the remaining years after an interruption; usable **only once** per taxpayer; may be combined with the option to aggregate. **EBF art. 58.º-A n.ºs 2, 5, 12.** https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/bf_rep/Pages/EBF58A.aspx
- **Eligibility:** the taxpayer must **not have been tax resident in Portugal in the previous 5 years**; must exercise one of the activities in art. 58.º-A n.º 1 alíneas (a)–(g); must **not benefit/have benefited from the old NHR**, **not opt for art. 12.º-A CIRS**, and not have previously used IFICI (n.º 10). Source: same URL + AT FAQ 5496: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/questoes_frequentes/pages/faqs-01018.aspx
- **Foreign-source income — important correction: the exemption is NOT in EBF art. 58.º-A.** It sits in **CIRS art. 81.º n.º 4:** IFICI beneficiaries obtaining **foreign-source income of categories A, B, E, F and G** get the **exemption method**, with **mandatory aggregation to determine the rate on remaining income** (so the exempt income still pushes up the rate). **Category H (pensions) is excluded** from the exemption (AT FAQ 5495). Income from **blacklisted jurisdictions is not exempt**: art. 81.º n.º 5 → **35%** (art. 71.º n.º 17 b)/c) and art. 72.º n.º 18). Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs81.aspx ; FAQ 5495: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/questoes_frequentes/pages/faqs-01018.aspx

  - **Ofício Circulado n.º 20276, de 26 de fevereiro de 2025** — AT guide + FAQs on IFICI (issued under Despacho SEAF n.º 37/2025-XXIV, de 25 de fevereiro). https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/instrucoes_administrativas/Documents/Oficio_circulado_20276_2025.pdf

### 6b. OLD NHR regime — status
- **Closed to new applicants.** Revoked by **Lei n.º 82/2023, de 29 de dezembro (OE2024), art. 263.º**, which deleted the NHR regime and added EBF art. 58.º-A (IFICI). https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/Lei_82_2023.pdf
- **Transitional regime — art. 236.º n.ºs 3 to 5 of Lei n.º 82/2023.** Old NHR rules (old arts. 16.º n.ºs 8–12, 22.º, 72.º n.ºs 10 and 12, 81.º n.ºs 4–8, 99.º n.º 8 and 101.º n.º 1 d) CIRS) continue to apply, until the end of the 10-year period counted **from the year the taxpayer became resident**, to taxpayers who are:
  (a) already registered as NHR at the law's entry into force; or
  (b) met the CIRS art. 16.º residence conditions on **31 December 2023**; or
  (c) **became tax resident by 31 December 2024** and declare one of: (i) **promise/contract of employment or secondment concluded by 31 Dec 2023** to be performed in Portugal; (ii) **lease or other contract granting use/possession of Portuguese property concluded by 10 Oct 2023**; (iii) **reservation or promise-to-purchase contract over Portuguese property concluded by 10 Oct 2023**; (iv) **enrolment of dependants in a Portuguese school completed by 10 Oct 2023**; (v) **residence visa/permit valid until 31 Dec 2023**; (vi) **residence-visa/permit procedure initiated by 31 Dec 2023**; or
  (d) is a **family member** of the above.
  Registration must be requested **electronically on Portal das Finanças after the resident-registration act, for the year of becoming resident** (n.º 4). Late registration → effects from the year of registration for the remaining period (n.º 5).
  Source (full nota with art. 236.º n.ºs 3–6 quoted): https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs72.aspx
- **So: transitional beneficiaries are the cohort that became resident no later than 31 December 2024; their 10-year NHR clock runs from the year they became resident (a 2024 arrival → benefit through 2033), not "2024 + 10" as a separate entitlement.** A commercial source claims the window closed definitively on **31 March 2025** — **UNVERIFIED as an official deadline**; the statutory last-arrival date is 31 Dec 2024 with registration for that year. (https://www.growinportugal.com/news/nhr-2-0-now-fully-selective-why-most-new-arrivals-no-longer-2026-09-03 — secondary source.)
- **No 2025 or 2026 law reopening or extending the old NHR was found**; OE2026 (Lei n.º 73-A/2025) contains no amendment to EBF art. 58.º-A (text search of the AT-published law).

### 6c. "Former resident" regime — art. 12.º-A CIRS (`regime dos antigos residentes`)
- **50% of category A (employment) and category B (business/professional) income is excluded from tax, capped at the upper limit of the first bracket of art. 68.º-A = €250,000, for 5 years.** Conditions: (a) **become tax resident (art. 16.º n.ºs 1–2) up to and including 2026**; (b) **not resident in Portugal in any of the previous 5 years**; (c) **were resident in Portugal in some period before that**; (d) tax situation regularised. Verbatim: CIRS art. 12.º-A n.º 1 (Redação da Lei n.º 82/2023, de 29 de dezembro). https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs12a.aspx
- The **€250,000 cap was introduced by OE2024 and applies only to taxpayers becoming resident in 2024 or later** (art. 236.º n.º 6 of Lei n.º 82/2023). Same source / art. 72.º page note.
- **Incompatible with IFICI** (IFICI art. 58.º-A n.º 10 al. b) excludes those opting for art. 12.º-A) and with the `IRS Jovem` regime (art. 12.º-B n.º 9 al. c)).
- No change found in OE2026.

## 7. Exit tax when LEAVING Portugal

**Bottom line: Portugal has a narrow individual exit tax (CIRS art. 10.º-A) plus a crypto deemed-disposal. There is NO general individual exit tax on holdings of >25% of a company, and no "financial assets above €X" exit charge. Those thresholds do not exist in the CIRS.**

- **CIRS art. 10.º-A — "Perda da qualidade de residente em território português".** When a *sócio* loses Portuguese tax residence, previously **deferred** gains become taxable in the year residence is lost. It applies to gains deferred under **art. 10.º n.ºs 8 and 9** (share-for-share exchange, merger, division, transfer of assets) and **art. 38.º**: taxed amount = market value of shares received less acquisition value of the old shares (etc.). Rate: **28%** (category G, art. 72.º n.º 1 al. c)). Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs10a.aspx
- **Deferral — art. 10.º-A n.º 3** (only where residence moves to **another EU or EEA Member State** bound by equivalent administrative cooperation in tax matters): (a) pay the whole assessment immediately with the return; (b) pay in the year in which each individual shareholding is extinguished or transferred (with an annual declaration); or (c) pay in **five equal annual instalments (one fifth per year)**. Options (b) and (c) **accrue interest at the late-payment rate** from the statutory due date. The option is exercised in the return for the year of loss of residence; an official declaration listing the shareholdings must be filed **by 31 August of the year following the transfer of residence**, and a **bank guarantee of the tax plus 25%** may be required. Same URL. (These deferral rules were added by **Decreto-Lei n.º 41/2016, de 1 de agosto**, in response to CJEU **Case C-503/14, Commission v Portugal, judgment of 21 December 2016**, which held the earlier Portuguese exit-tax rules in arts. 10.º and 38.º CIRS incompatible with EU law.) CJEU: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:62014CJ0503
- **Confirmation there is no broader exit tax for individuals:** the CIRS index lists **only one** article under "Perda da qualidade de residente em território português" — art. 10.º-A (https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/codigo-do-irs-indice.aspx); art. 10.º n.º 1 (alíneas a–k) contains **no deemed disposal on emigration** except for crypto; and PwC's Portugal individual summary lists **no exit tax**. https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs10.aspx
- **Crypto exit tax — CIRS art. 10.º n.º 25 (current numbering; n.º 22 before Decreto-Lei n.º 97/2026):** "a perda da qualidade de residente em território português é equiparada a uma alienação onerosa" for crypto-assets → gains taxed at 28% at the date of departure (≥365-day exemption unavailable because departure is a deemed disposal). Same art. 10.º URL.
- **No entry step-up** (see §5).
- **>25% shareholdings:** the only 25% threshold in this area is the **CFC rule in CIRS art. 20.º n.º 3** (imputation of profits of non-resident entities in privileged regimes where the taxpayer holds ≥25%) — it is **not** an exit tax. Text: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/Lei_31_2024.pdf (art. 2.º amending art. 20.º CIRS).
- No 2025/2026 amendment to art. 10.º-A was found.

## 8. Annual minimum tax / lump-sum floor at moderate wealth

- **No minimum income tax and no lump-sum floor for individuals.** There is no net wealth tax (§4), and AIMI only bites above **€600,000** of aggregate urban-property VPT (art. 135.º-C n.º 2 al. a) CIMI).
- **Floor-type rules that do exist:**
  - **`Mínimo de existência` — CIRS art. 70.º n.º 1:** reference value = the **greater of €12,880 and 1.5 × 14 × IAS** (art. 70.º as currently shown by the AT, citing "Redação da Lei n.º 73-A/2024, de 30 de dezembro"). At IAS 2025 (€522.50) → 1.5 × 14 × 522.50 = €10,972.50, so **€12,880** governs; at IAS 2026 (€537.13) → €11,279.73, so **€12,880** governs. It is a mechanism that exempts subsistence income, not a minimum tax. https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs70.aspx — **CAUTION: the amending-law year cited on the AT page is internally inconsistent (see UNVERIFIED list).**
  - **Solidarity surcharge** (art. 68.º-A) only above €80,000 — a top-up, not a floor.
  - **Self-employed minimum contributory base** of €20/month where the relevant income is below that: **UNVERIFIED for 2025/2026** (secondary references only).

## UNVERIFIED list

1. **Social-security contribution ceiling for employees.** No official confirmation of any maximum contributory base for 2025/2026. A 2011 secondary source cites "12 × IAS" (= €6,270/month in 2025; €6,445.56/month in 2026), but the current Código dos Regimes Contributivos (Lei n.º 110/2009, art. 44.º) could not be retrieved (dre.pt/pgdlisboa/seg-social attempts failed: the Código Contributivo PDF mirror returned 0 bytes and the Segurança Social guide is behind an auth wall). **Do not assert a ceiling without direct verification.**
2. **AIMI low-income exemption threshold (commonly quoted €153,300)** — the CIMI art. 135.º-E page returned no text; unconfirmed for 2025/2026.
3. **Date of Portaria n.º 52-A/2025/1** — Ofício Circulado 20278/2025 says 25 February; Ofício Circulado 20276/2025 says 25 January. One is a typo in the AT's own documents.
4. **Date/year of the law that last amended CIRS art. 70.º (mínimo de existência, €12,880)** — the AT page cites "Lei n.º 73-A/2024, de 30 de dezembro", which does not correspond to a known OE (OE2025 = Lei 45-A/2024 which set €12,180; OE2026 = Lei 73-A/2025, de 30 de dezembro). Likely an AT typo, but the €12,880 figure itself is taken from the official AT text.
5. **Exact official closing date of the NHR transitional registration window (a commercial source says 31 March 2025).** The statute (art. 236.º n.ºs 3–4 of Lei n.º 82/2023) fixes the last arrival date at 31 December 2024 with registration "por referência ao ano em que se tornou residente"; the AT leaflet for RNH registration is in French and its footnotes did not extract cleanly. Also, whether any administrative extension applied to NHR (as it did for IFICI's 2024 income, to 31 March 2025) is unconfirmed.
6. **Practical scope of the art. 43.º n.º 5 long-term exclusion for foreign-source securities.** Two credible sources contradict each other (Abreu, 6 Oct 2025: AT confined it to domestic-annex disposals; eco.sapo.pt: the holding period counts for foreign funds declared in Annex J). The statute itself is issuer-neutral.
7. **Self-employed minimum monthly contributory base (€20)** for 2025/2026 — secondary sources only.
8. **Whether the 2025 rate table (Lei n.º 55-A/2025) applies to the whole of income year 2025 or only from 23 July 2025.** The law enters into force the day after publication and contains no retroactivity clause; the AT issued new withholding tables on 22 July 2025 to align annual withholding, which is the mechanism used when the annual table is meant to govern the full year. Not stated expressly in the law.
