## Ireland

- **`s.579A TCA 1997` is NOT an exit tax.** Title verified: **"Attribution of gains to beneficiaries"** — offshore-settlement trust gains attributed to beneficiaries. Sources: [charteredaccountants.ie TaxSource Total, s.579A full text](https://www.charteredaccountants.ie/taxsourcetotal/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec0579A.html); [Revenue Note for Guidance, s.579A](https://www.charteredaccountants.ie/taxsourcetotal/1997/en/act/pub/0039/nfg/sec0579A-nfg.html). **The brief's premise ("s.579A = charge on unrealised gains for individuals resident 5+ years who cease residence") is incorrect on both the section number and the mechanism.**
- **There is no "resident 5+ years then leave" charge.** The only "5" is the **5-year absence window** in **s.29A TCA 1997 "Temporary non-residents"**.
- **s.29A(1)(a) — "relevant assets" (verbatim, 2026):** *"shares in a company, or rights to acquire shares in a company, being shares or rights which he or she beneficially owned on the last day of the year of his or her departure and the market value of which on that day — (i) is equal to, or exceeds, 5 per cent of the value of the issued share capital of the company, or (ii) exceeds €500,000"* ([full text](https://www.charteredaccountants.ie/taxsourcetotal/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec0029A.html)).
- **s.29A(2) — conditions (verbatim):** the individual has relevant assets; is resident for the "year of return"; *"was not resident in the State for one or more years of assessment immediately preceding the year of his or her return; but there is a year of assessment before the year of return for which the individual was resident in the State and, at any time during that year, the individual was domiciled in the State"*; and *"there are not more than 5 years of assessment falling between the year of his or her departure and the year of his or her return."*
- **s.29A(3), (3A), (4) — mechanism:** on disposal in an intervening year the individual is **deemed to have disposed of and immediately reacquired** the assets **on the last day of the year of departure** at that day's market value; (3A) substitutes the **actual** disposal market value where different; (4) allows a **foreign tax credit** for tax payable in a treaty territory.
- **Revenue guidance:** [Revenue TDM Part 02-03-02, "Temporary non-residents (S.29A)", reviewed December 2024](https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/tdm/income-tax-capital-gains-tax-corporation-tax/part-02/02-03-02.pdf) — confirms the assets are *"a holding of the issued share capital in any company (wherever located) with a value of either 5% or more of that company's issued share capital or exceeding €500,000"*, that the charge arises only where the person is *"not taxable in the State for a period of no more than 5 years of assessment before again becoming so taxable"* and *"were domiciled in Ireland prior to departure"*, and that FA 2014 s.46 amended the market value used.
- **Ireland's real Exit Tax is corporate.** [Revenue, "Exit Tax provisions" (page published 23 June 2026)](https://www.revenue.ie/en/companies-and-charities/anti-beps-measures/exit-tax-provisions.aspx): s.627–629C TCA 1997, **Chapter 2 of Part 20**, implementing **Article 5 of EU Directive 2016/1164 (ATAD)**; *"The rate of Exit Tax is 12.5%"*; *"Companies may defer the immediate payment of Exit Tax by electing to pay it in equal instalments over five years"*; applies to **events on or after 10 October 2018**; carve-outs for securities-financing/security assets and for prudential-capital/liquidity transfers reverting within 12 months; no charge where Ireland retains taxing rights or assets remain used in Ireland through a PE. Revenue's guidance manual: [TDM Part 20-02-01, Exit Tax Provisions (S.627–S.629C)](https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/tdm/income-tax-capital-gains-tax-corporation-tax/part-20/20-02-01.pdf).
- **Rates 2025/2026.** CGT **33%** in both years: *"no changes were made to the headline rate of capital gains tax (CGT) at 33%"* — [Saffery, "Budget 2026 at a glance"](https://www.saffery.com/ie/insights/articles/budget-2026-at-a-glance/).
- **2026 legislated change — life policies and funds.** [Finance Act 2025, s.37 "Life assurance policies and investment funds"](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2025/act/18/section/37/enacted/en/html) substitutes **"38 per cent" for "41 per cent"** in ss.730F(1)(a)(ii), 730J(a)(i)(II), 730K(1)(a)(ii), 739D(5A) formula `(G x 41) → (G x 38)`, 739E(1)(a)(ii) and (b)(ii), 747D(a)(i)(II), 747E(1)(b)(ii). **s.37(2): each amendment applies to chargeable events / receipts / disposals "on or after 1 January 2026."** Corroboration: [Saffery](https://www.saffery.com/ie/insights/articles/budget-2026-at-a-glance/) (*"reduce from the current 41% rate to 38% from 1 January 2026"*). The **41%** 2025 figure is also stated in the Dáil record: *"Exit tax applies at a rate of 41% in respect of Irish resident individual investors"* ([Oireachtas PQ, 20 March 2025](https://www.oireachtas.ie/en/debates/question/2025-03-20/section/256/)).
- **Budget 2026 other relevant measure:** Revised Entrepreneur Relief lifetime limit **EUR 1m → EUR 1.5m from 1 January 2026**; a roadmap to simplify investment-tax reporting expected in 2026 ([Saffery](https://www.saffery.com/ie/insights/articles/budget-2026-at-a-glance/)).

## Italy

- **Rule and citation (2026):** **art. 166 TUIR** (DPR 917/1986), "Trasferimento all'estero della residenza" / *imposizione in uscita*. Current form **in force since 12 January 2019**, introduced by **art. 2 D.Lgs. 29 novembre 2018, n. 142**, implementing **ATAD 2016/1164/EU** ([leggeinchiaro, "Art. 166 TUIR: Imposizione in uscita"](https://leggeinchiaro.it/articolo-166-del-tuir/)).
- **MAJOR 2026 DEVELOPMENT — the whole old TUIR was abrogated.** Normattiva now shows **"PROVVEDIMENTO ABROGATO DAL D.LGS. 19 GIUGNO 2026, N. 117"** on articles of DPR 917/1986 — verified independently on [art. 1](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:presidente.repubblica:decreto:1986-12-22;917~art1), [art. 166](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:presidente.repubblica:decreto:1986-12-22;917~art166) and [art. 24-bis](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:presidente.repubblica:decreto:1986-12-22;917~art24bis). Replacement: **D.Lgs. 19 giugno 2026, n. 117 — "Testo unico delle disposizioni legislative in materia di imposte sui redditi"**, **Gazzetta Ufficiale n. 132 of 3 July 2026**, **operative from 1 January 2027** ([EC News](https://www.ecnews.it/lavoro/imposte-contributi-e-premi/tassazione-e-redditi-di-lavoro/in-gazzetta-il-nuovo-tuir-in-vigore-dal-1-gennaio-2027/); decree record [edizionieuropee](http://www.edizionieuropee.it/law/html/225/zn95_21_388.html)). **Effect: art. 166 TUIR governs 2026; from 1 Jan 2027 the exit tax is art. 193 of the new TUIR.**
- **New article number — art. 193 "Imposizione in uscita"** (text verified, verbatim opening): *"Le disposizioni del presente articolo si applicano ai soggetti che esercitano imprese commerciali qualora si verifichi una delle seguenti ipotesi: a) sono fiscalmente residenti nel territorio dello Stato e trasferiscono all'estero la propria residenza fiscale; b) … trasferiscono attivi a una loro stabile organizzazione situata all'estero con riferimento alla quale si applica l'esenzione degli utili e delle perdite di cui all'articolo 184; c) … trasferiscono l'intera stabile organizzazione alla sede centrale o ad altra stabile organizzazione situata all'estero; d) … trasferiscono attivi facenti parte del patrimonio di tale stabile organizzazione …; e) … sono stati oggetto di incorporazione da parte di una società fiscalmente non residente oppure hanno effettuato una scissione a favore di una o più beneficiarie non residenti oppure hanno effettuato il conferimento di una stabile organizzazione o di un ramo di essa situati all'estero a favore di un soggetto fiscalmente residente all'estero."* ([new TUIR art. 193, miolegale.it](https://miolegale.it/norme/tuir-dlgs-117-2026/)). Case **(e)** is new relative to art. 166's four classic cases.
- **Does it apply to individuals?** Only insofar as the person **exercises a commercial business** ("soggetti che esercitano imprese commerciali"). Art. 166's four cases: transfer abroad of tax residence (companies/entities); transfer of assets to a foreign PE enjoying the **branch exemption** (art. 168-ter TUIR); transfer abroad of an entire Italian PE of a foreign company; transfer of assets of an Italian PE to head office/another foreign PE ([leggeinchiaro](https://leggeinchiaro.it/articolo-166-del-tuir/)). For **sole proprietorships and partnerships**, art. 166 provided: *"Per le imprese individuali e le società di persone si applica l'articolo 17, comma 1, lettere g) e l)"* — **separate taxation** of the deemed gain ([art. 166 text, trovalegge.it](https://trovalegge.it/tu-imposte-redditi/art-166-tuir-trasferimento-all-estero-della-residenza)). **No general Italian exit tax on a departing individual's private portfolio shareholdings**; art. 166(2-ter) expressly states a company's change of residence *"non dà luogo di per sé all'imposizione dei soci della società trasferita"* ([trovalegge.it](https://trovalegge.it/tu-imposte-redditi/art-166-tuir-trasferimento-all-estero-della-residenza)).
- **Tax base:** market value at transfer date minus tax-recognised cost; market value determined on arm's-length terms (art. 110(7) criteria), **including goodwill** for a business or branch; asset classes include immovables, participations, patents, trademarks, know-how, goodwill, movable business assets, inventory. Uncompensated losses may be offset against the exit gain under conditions in the new art. 193(6). **PEX** (art. 87 TUIR / art. 184 new TUIR) → only **5%** taxed ([leggeinchiaro](https://leggeinchiaro.it/articolo-166-del-tuir/); [new art. 193](https://miolegale.it/norme/tuir-dlgs-117-2026/)).
- **Deferral:** option to pay in **five equal annual instalments** where the transfer is to an EU or EEA State guaranteeing adequate information exchange and recovery assistance (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein under specific agreements); guarantees may be required ([leggeinchiaro](https://leggeinchiaro.it/articolo-166-del-tuir/)). The earlier mechanism was a **suspension of the realisation's effects** for EU/EEA transfers — art. 166(2-quater)/(2-quinquies), added by **D.L. 24 gennaio 2012, n. 1**, expressly implementing **CJEU C-371/10 *National Grid Indus BV*** ([trovalegge.it](https://trovalegge.it/tu-imposte-redditi/art-166-tuir-trasferimento-all-estero-della-residenza)).
- **Flat tax for new residents — art. 24-bis TUIR, raised for 2026.** Per [Fisco Oggi (Agenzia delle Entrate)](https://www.fiscooggi.it/portale/-/regime-agevolato-dei-neo-residenti-maggiorati-gli-importi-forfettari): individuals transferring tax residence to Italy **after 1 January 2026** may opt to pay a substitute tax of *"300mila euro anziché 200mila euro annui previsti dalla norma previgente"* on foreign income irrespective of amount, and family members *"50mila euro anziché 25mila euro"*; made *"con una modifica dell'articolo 24-bis, comma 2, primo periodo, del Tuir (Dpr n. 917/1986)"*. Headline corroboration: [Fiscal Focus, "Neo-residenti: Bilancio 2026 alza l'imposta forfettaria sui redditi esteri"](https://www.fiscal-focus.it/news-24/ore-16-00-neo-residenti-bilancio-2026-alza-l-imposta-forfettaria-sui-redditi-esteri,3,180604).
- **Labelled figures:** **2024–2025 = EUR 200,000 / EUR 25,000**; **2026 = EUR 300,000 / EUR 50,000** (transfers after 1 Jan 2026). The original **2017 EUR 100,000** figure is **UNVERIFIED in this run**.
- **Note:** art. 24-bis TUIR is also abrogated by D.Lgs. 117/2026, so the neo-resident regime will be restated in the new TUIR from 1 Jan 2027 — **new article number UNVERIFIED**.

## Portugal

- **Direct answer: NO general personal IRS exit tax exists in Portugal as of 2026.** There is **no rule treating emigration as a deemed disposal** of an individual's shares, securities, real estate or general portfolio. [Dixcart/Mondaq, "Navigating Portugal's Exit Tax" (21 October 2025)](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/trusts/1694424/navigating-portugals-exit-tax-a-guide-for-individuals-and-businesses): *"Unlike countries that tax a departing resident's entire portfolio, Portugal's exit tax on private individuals is not a broad levy on unrealised gains from assets like real estate or general stocks."*
- **art. 10.º-A CIRS — "Perda da qualidade de residente em território português" — STILL IN FORCE in 2026, but narrow.** Verbatim opening (official text): *"Nos casos referidos nos n.os 8 e 9 do artigo anterior, e, bem assim, no artigo 38.º, perdendo o sócio a qualidade de residente em território português, há lugar à consideração enquanto mais-valias, para efeitos da tributação respeitante ao ano em que se verificar aquela perda da qualidade de residente, do valor que, por virtude do disposto naqueles números e artigo, não foi tributado aquando da permuta de ações, da fusão ou da cisão ou de transmissão do património…"* — i.e. it recaptures gains **previously deferred under the share-exchange / merger / division / transfer-of-patrimony neutrality rules** ([Portal das Finanças, CIRS art. 10.º-A](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs10a.aspx)). Official attribution note on the same page: *"artigo aditado pela Lei n.º 82-E/2014, de 31 de dezembro"*, subsequently amended by **Decreto-Lei n.º 41/2016, de 1 de agosto**.
- **"More favourable" rules for EU/EEA moves — art. 10.º-A(3) (official text).** Where loss of residence results from moving to **another EU or EEA Member State** bound by administrative cooperation in tax matters equivalent to the EU framework, the tax on the positive balance is payable **(a) immediately in full** in the return filed under arts. 57 and 60; **(b) in the year following the extinction or transfer, by any title, of each individually identified holding**, for the part of tax corresponding to that holding's fiscal result; or **(c) in equal annual instalments corresponding to one fifth** of the tax computed in the year of transfer. Choosing (b) or (c) **accrues interest at the late-payment rate** from the day after the art. 97(1) due date. The option is exercised in the return for the year residence was lost and requires an official schedule **by 31 August of the year following the transfer**; a **bank guarantee of tax + 25%** may be required where collection is at risk. **Moving to a non-EU/EEA country requires immediate payment of the whole tax, or of outstanding instalments** ([Portal das Finanças](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs10a.aspx)).
- **Crypto — the one genuine individual departure charge, renumbered for 2026.** CIRS art. 10.º **n.º 25** (2026): *"Para efeitos do disposto na alínea k) do n.º 1, a perda da qualidade de residente em território português é equiparada a uma alienação onerosa."* Official attribution: *"(anterior n.º 22) — Redação do Decreto-Lei n.º 97/2026, de 20 de maio"* ([Portal das Finanças, CIRS art. 10.º](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs10.aspx)).
  - **2026 renumbering produces effects from 1 January 2026** — verified: *"Renumerado pelo Decreto-Lei n.º 97/2026, de 20 de maio … Produz efeitos a partir de 1 de janeiro de 2026"* ([PwC, Coletânea Tributária Anotada 2026 — DL 97/2026](https://www.pwc.pt/pt/pwcinforfisco/coletanea-tributaria-anotada/2026/pwc-coletanea-tributaria-ebf-irs-iva-imt-dl97-2026.pdf)).
  - **Holding-period exemption, art. 10.º n.º 22 (2026; formerly n.º 19):** gains and losses on crypto disposals are **excluded where the crypto was held ≥365 days**. **Art. 10.º n.º 21 (formerly n.º 18)** excludes **non-fungible crypto assets** ([Portal das Finanças](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs10.aspx)).
  - **Rate 28%**, on departure market value − acquisition cost, **FIFO** — **secondary source only** ([Dixcart/Mondaq, 21 Oct 2025](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/trusts/1694424/navigating-portugals-exit-tax-a-guide-for-individuals-and-businesses)); not confirmed against art. 72.º CIRS.
- **Corporate exit tax — art. 83.º CIRC, "Transferência de residência".** On cessation of activity resulting from transfer of residence abroad, positive/negative differences between **market value and tax-relevant value of the entity's assets** (even if not in the accounts) are taxable components. For transfers **to another EU Member State or an EEA State with equivalent mutual assistance in recovery**, payment may be (a) all immediately; **b) REVOKED** by **Lei n.º 32/2019, de 3 de maio**; **(c) five equal annual instalments**, with late-payment interest; option exercised in the return for the cessation period, with an official schedule and possible **bank guarantee of tax + 25%** ([Portal das Finanças, CIRC art. 83.º](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc83.aspx)).
- **Anti-abuse rule (not an exit tax):** a Portuguese national moving to a country/territory/region with a *"clearly more favourable tax regime"* may remain Portuguese resident for the year of change **and the four subsequent years**, unless the move is for a valid reason; it ends when they become resident in a non-listed country ([Dixcart/Mondaq, 21 Oct 2025](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/trusts/1694424/navigating-portugals-exit-tax-a-guide-for-individuals-and-businesses)). **Exact CIRS article: UNVERIFIED.**
- **IFICI (successor to NHR) — 2025/2026 status.** Individuals who become Portuguese resident and **were not tax resident in Portugal in the previous 5 years** may apply, for a **10-year consecutive period**, for a **20% flat rate** on employment income (category A) and professional/self-employment income (category B) from listed activities — higher-education teaching & scientific research; qualified jobs in entities within contractual investment benefits under the Investment Tax Code; highly qualified professions listed in **Ordinance no. 352/2024/1, of 23 December** ([Garrigues, "Key Points on Portugal's IFICI Tax Incentive", March 2025](https://www.garrigues.com/sites/default/files/noticias/files/portugal-key-points-on-portugal-s-ifici-tax-incentive.pdf)). Corroborated by [PwC, Guia Fiscal 2026 – IRS](https://www.pwc.pt/pt/pwcinforfisco/guia-fiscal/2026/irs.html): *"São tributados a uma taxa especial de 20% sobre os rendimentos líquidos do trabalho dependente (categoria A) e empresariais e profissionais (categoria B)"*; foreign-source income may be exempt (10% for pensions). Prior registration with the competent body (FCT for research; AICEP for investment-contract jobs) is required ([Garrigues](https://www.garrigues.com/sites/default/files/noticias/files/portugal-key-points-on-portugal-s-ifici-tax-incentive.pdf)).
- **2023/2024/2025/2026 Budget changes to the exit tax:** **no source found showing repeal of art. 10.º-A CIRS**; the article remains published as current on Portal das Finanças in 2026. Any *"2023/2024 repeal or narrowing of the personal exit tax"* is **UNVERIFIED — no supporting primary source located.**

## Switzerland

- **No federal exit tax for individuals — verified from statute.** **DBG (SR 642.11) Art. 16 Abs. 3:** *"Die Kapitalgewinne aus der Veräusserung von Privatvermögen sind steuerfrei."* ([lawbrary.ch, DBG Art. 16, version 2021.07](https://lawbrary.ch/law/art/DBG-v2021.07-de-art-16/)). Cantonal analogue, e.g. Zurich: *"Die Kapitalgewinne aus der Veräusserung von Privatvermögen sind steuerfrei"* ([ZHlex, Zürcher Steuergesetz](http://www2.zhlex.zh.ch/Appl/zhlex_r.nsf/OpenAttachment?Open&docid=151C0BBF6E29D239C1256F47002B4706&file=631.1_8.6.97_47.pdf)). Consequence: **no deemed disposal on emigration** of an individual's shares, securities or other private movable assets.
- **ATAD does not bind Switzerland** (third country), so there is no EU-law-driven exit tax there; the ATAD exit-tax rules bind only EU Member States ([University of Zurich LL.M. Tax, "The Impact of the ATAD on Domestic Systems"](https://www.llmtax.uzh.ch/dam/jcr:4ce14695-e56f-425a-8aeb-d64f04f995c9/Beilage%2011%20Opel%20ATAD.pdf)).
- **De facto departure charge #1 — business assets (Geschäftsvermögen).** *"Bei der Aufgabe der selbständigen Erwerbstätigkeit verbleiben die nicht privat genutzten Vermögensgegenstände im Geschäftsvermögen. Sie gehen durch den blossen Zeitablauf nicht ins Privatvermögen über; die bei der späteren Veräusserung erzielten Gewinne werden als Einkommen aus selbständiger Erwerbstätigkeit besteuert"* — **BGE 125 II 113**, reproduced on [lawbrary.ch DBG Art. 16](https://lawbrary.ch/law/art/DBG-v2021.07-de-art-16/). Federal practice on realisation/liquidation gains: [ESTV, Steuerpraxis "Selbständige Erwerbstätigkeit"](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/d-selbstaendige-erwerbstaetigkeit-de.pdf) (sections **4.2 "Realisation"** — Begriff / Buchmässige / Echte Realisation; **4.3 "Liquidationsgewinne"**; **4.5.2 "Ersatzbeschaffung"**). **Thus latent gains on business assets enter charge as ordinary self-employment income on cessation/emigration — this is the "latente Steuer" outcome in practice, not a separate exit-tax statute.**
- **De facto departure charge #2 — real estate.** Swiss real-estate gains bear the **cantonal Grundstückgewinnsteuer**; the **canton of situs retains taxing rights**, and international/intercantonal apportionment for real estate, business operations and permanent establishments is set out in [Zürcher Steuerbuch Nr. 5.3, "Internationale Steuerausscheidung bei Grundstücken, Geschäftsbetrieben und Betriebsstätten" (vom 5. März 2026)](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-5-3/zstb-nr-5-3.pdf). **A specific 5- or 10-year retention rule on emigration is UNVERIFIED in this run.**
- **Lump-sum taxation (Aufwandbesteuerung / Pauschalbesteuerung / forfait fiscal) — basis and 2026 figures.** **DBG Art. 14:** available to natural persons who (a) **do not hold Swiss citizenship**, (b) are **unlimitedly liable for the first time or after at least a ten-year interruption**, and (c) **exercise no gainful activity in Switzerland**. The base is the **highest** of: **(a) CHF 400,000**; **(b) for taxpayers with their own household, seven times the annual rent or the rental value under art. 21(1)(b)**; **(c) for other taxpayers, three times the annual pension price for board and lodging**; **(d) the sum of Swiss-source gross income** (immovables, movables, invested capital, copyrights/patents, pensions, etc.) ([lawbrary.ch, DBG Art. 14](https://lawbrary.ch/law/art/DBG-v2021.07-de-art-14/); text: *"a. 400 000 Franken"*, footnote: in force since **1 January 2016** per AS 2013 779 / AS 2013 2397).
  - **2026 federal figure applied — CHF 435,000.** [Bär & Karrer, "Aktuelles zum internationalen Steuerrecht — Aufwandbesteuerung", deck dated 12 March 2026](https://www.baerkarrer.ch/userdata/files/26-402-1-isis-webinar-istr-brozzo-kobiersi-aufwandbesteuerung.pdf): *"Mindestpauschalen Einkommen 2026 … **Bund 435'000**"*. Same figure in the official cantonal memo [Amt Uri, "Merkblatt über die Besteuerung nach dem Aufwand (gültig ab 1. Januar 2026)"](https://www.ur.ch/_docn/439772/14_Merkblatt_Aufwandbesteuerung_01.01.2026_1.pdf): *"Mindestbemessungsgrundlage: 435'000 Franken; das 7-fache des jährlichen Mietzinses oder Mietwerts; das 3-fache des jährlichen Pensionspreises."*
  - **Cantonal minimum lump-sum income 2026** (Bär & Karrer, 12 March 2026): **LU 600,000 · SG 600,000 · SZ 600,000 · ZG 500,000 · VD 450,800 · GR 435,000 · TI 435,000 · UR 435,000 · GL 435,000 · GE 426,357 · AG 400,000 · BE 400,000 · AI 400,000 · NW 400,000 · OW 400,000 · NE 400,000 · SO 400,000 · VS 250,000 · FR 250,000 · JU 200,000 · TG 150,000.** Minimum wealth bases include **20× income** (AG, GR, SZ, AI, LU, UR, NW, …), **8× income** (JU), **5× income** (TI, NE), **150,000** (TG), and **"BELIG zum tl. Wert"** (BE). **ZH, BS, BL, SH and AR: n/a — lump-sum taxation not offered.**
- **2026 change — individual taxation.** The **Bundesgesetz über die Individualbesteuerung** (federal law of **20 June 2025**) was **approved in the referendum of 8 March 2026** ([admin.ch vote page, 8 March 2026](https://www.admin.ch/gov/en/start/documentation/votes/20260308/imposizione-individuale.html); referendum record [swissvotes 685.00](https://swissvotes.ch/vote/685.00/zustandekommen-de.pdf)). Effect on lump-sum taxation, per [Bär & Karrer (12 March 2026)](https://www.baerkarrer.ch/userdata/files/26-402-1-isis-webinar-istr-brozzo-kobiersi-aufwandbesteuerung.pdf): *"Jeder Ehegatte wird separat besteuert · Neu kann somit ein Ehepartner ordentlich und der andere nach dem Aufwand besteuert werden · Steuerbemessung für beide separat. Somit **Verdoppelung Mindestbemessungsgrundlage, wenn beide pauschaliert**."*
- **Lump-sum abolition initiative — last federal vote, and 2026 status.** The federal popular initiative **«Schluss mit den Steuerprivilegien für Millionäre (Abschaffung der Pauschalbesteuerung)»**, submitted **19 October 2012**, was **rejected on 30 November 2014**: **1,528,114 No vs 1,053,125 Yes**, and by the cantons **19 6/2 No vs 1 Yes** — official: [Bundesratsbeschluss über das Ergebnis der Volksabstimmung vom 30. November 2014, BBl 2015 1813 (fedlex FGA 2015/317, PDF)](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/filestore/fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/fga/2015/317/de/pdf-a/fedlex-data-admin-ch-eli-fga-2015-317-de-pdf-a.pdf); vote portal [bk.admin.ch, 30.11.2014](https://www.bk.admin.ch/ch/d/pore/va/20141130/). **No pending federal abolition initiative for 2025/2026 was identified — see Uncertain.** (For context, the Swiss federal vote of late 2025 rejected an **inheritance-tax** initiative: [Baker McKenzie, December 2025](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/12/switzerland-swiss-voters-reject-federal-inheritance-tax-initiative).)
- **Historical (not 2025/26):** the admin.ch pages *"Bundesrat will Aufwandbesteuerung erhöhen"* (**8 September 2010**) and *"Bundesrat will Aufwandbesteuerung beibehalten, aber verschärfen"* (**30 June 2011**) document the reform that produced the **7× / CHF 400,000** standard ([2010](https://www.admin.ch/de/nsb?id=35029); [2011](https://www.admin.ch/de/nsb?id=39966)) — they are **not** current developments.
