## 2. Primary-source confirmations

### Italy — HNWI flat tax **[P]** Agenzia delle Entrate (link above)
- Substitute tax on foreign income: **€300,000/yr** for individuals transferring tax residence to Italy **from 1 Jan 2026** (Budget Law 2026).
- Per family member electing extension: **€50,000/yr** (was €25,000).
- Pre-2026 entrants: €200,000 (residence transferred 10 Aug 2024 – 31 Dec 2025); €100,000 (before 10 Aug 2024).
- Eligibility: non-resident in Italy for ≥9 of the preceding 10 tax periods.
- Duration: **15 years** from first year of validity; tacitly renewed annually; revocable.
- Paid in one instalment, no *ravvedimento operoso* (no late-payment leniency). Tax code **NRPP**.
- Grandfathering provision exists for existing optants per **[A]** [Charles Russell Speechlys](https://www.charlesrussellspeechlys.com/en/insights/quick-reads/lump-sum-tax-regime-higher-annual-flat-tax-and-grandfathering-provision/) *(detail unverified — page body not retrievable)*.

### UK — FIG regime **[P]** [HMRC HS266 (2026), updated 18 May 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/foreign-income-and-gains-fig-regime-self-assessment-helpsheet-hs266/hs266-foreign-income-and-gains-fig-regime-2026)
- From **6 April 2025**: remittance basis **abolished**; domicile removed as a connecting factor, replaced by a residence-based system. All UK residents taxed on the **arising basis** on worldwide income and gains.
- **FIG regime**: "qualifying new resident" = one of first **4 years** of UK residence after **≥10 consecutive tax years of non-UK residence**; not a member of the Commons/Lords.
- Relief by claim **per year, per source**; unused years **cannot** be rolled over.
- **No cap** on the amount of relief: HMRC states "There is no limit to the amount of relief which may be claimed".
- Bring relieved income/gains to the UK with **no** UK tax liability.
- Remittance-basis-era entrants: can use FIG from 2025-26 if UK resident from 2022-23 onwards.
- **Cost of claiming**: lose personal allowance, CGT annual exempt amount, blind person's allowance, marriage allowance, life-insurance/trade-union reliefs; foreign losses unavailable (no carry-forward/back); FTCR not available on relieved income; pension relief limited.
- Pre-6 Apr 2025 income/gains: **not** eligible; may use **Temporary Repatriation Facility (TRF)** — HS264.
- Claim deadline: 31 Jan following the tax year +12 months (i.e. 31 Jan **2028** for 2025-26).
- Disqualified income includes performance income, certain pension income, settlements-legislation income, transferred income streams.

### Australia — CGT reform detail **[A]** [StewartBrown summarising CA ANZ/Thompson Reuters, 13 May 2026](https://www.stewartbrown.com.au/tax-articles/2026-federal-budget-tax-reform-what-this-means-for-you)
- **From 1 July 2027**: 50% CGT discount replaced by **cost-base indexation** (assets held >12 months) **+ 30% minimum tax on net capital gains**.
- Applies to **all** CGT assets, **including pre-1985 assets**, held by individuals, trusts and partnerships.
- **Transitional**: only gains **arising on/after 1 Jul 2027** are affected; the 50% discount still applies to gains arising before then. Gains on pre-1985 assets arising before 1 Jul 2027 remain exempt.
- New residential property investors may **elect** either the 50% discount OR indexation + minimum tax.
- Income-support recipients (incl. Age Pension) **exempt** from the minimum tax.
- Related: negative gearing limited to new builds from 1 Jul 2027 (established dwellings acquired from 7:30pm AEST 12 May 2026); **30% minimum tax on discretionary trusts from 1 Jul 2028**; $250 Working Australians Tax Offset from 2027-28; $1,000 instant work-expense deduction from 2026-27.
- ⚠️ **Date conflict**: [regfollower](https://regfollower.com/australia-enacts-twin-tax-reform-acts-overhauls-cgt-discount-adds-minimum-tax-rate-on-capital-gains/) says the two Acts are "largely effective from 1 July 2026". Resolution: the **non-CGT** measures (instant deduction) start 1 Jul 2026; the **CGT** measures start **1 Jul 2027**. Multiple independent sources (CA ANZ, E&P, Hudson) give 1 Jul 2027 for CGT. Treat 1 Jul 2027 as the CGT date.

### Greece — three regimes in the Income Tax Code **[A]** [IBA practical guide, T. Skouzos, 13 Nov 2024](https://prod-bo.ibanet.org/preferential-tax-regimes-for-individuals-moving-their-tax-residency-to-Greece); corroborated for 2026 by [Ipanema Partners, updated Apr 2026](https://ipanemapartners.com/en/insights/greece-golden-visa-tax)
- **Art. 5A (non-dom)**: lump-sum **€100,000/yr** exhausting total tax liability on **all foreign-sourced income including capital gains**, regardless of amount. No obligation to declare foreign income in Greece. Paid in one instalment (last working day of July; first year within 30 days of acceptance). **Max 15 tax years** incl. year of application. Family members **+€20,000/yr each** (children free). **Foreign property exempt from Greek inheritance & gift tax** during the regime. **No** foreign tax credit. Greek-source income taxed under general rules.
  - Eligibility: not Greek tax resident for **7 of the 8** years before transfer; must invest **≥€500,000** within 3 years (real estate in Greece; unlisted Greek corporate shares; Greek govt bonds with ≥3yr remaining maturity via a Greek credit institution; Greek AIF participation; listed Greek shares/bonds/ETF units). Investment **not** required if holding a qualifying investment-activity residence permit. Apply by **31 March**.
- **Art. 5B (foreign pensioner)**: **7%** flat on total foreign-source income (not limited to pension). Max **15 years**. **No** family extension. **No** foreign-property inheritance exemption. Foreign tax credit **allowed**. Eligibility: not resident 5 of prior 6 years.
- **Art. 5C (employee/self-employed)**: **50% exemption** on Greek-source employment/business income, remainder taxed normally. Max **7 tax years**. Exempt from deemed income on residence/car. May be combined with 5A or 5B. Eligibility: not resident 5 of prior 6 years; transfer from EU/EEA or admin-cooperation state; new employment/board role/PE executive or new Greek self-employed activity; declare intent to stay ≥2 years.
- Greece: top PIT **44%**; CIT **22%**; **no net wealth tax**; annual property tax ENFIA applies. Foreign capital gains for non-doms are absorbed in the €100k.
- ⚠️ Ipanema's comparison table states Italy's flat tax as €100k — **outdated/wrong** (it is €300k from 1 Jan 2026). Use IBA for legal structure, Agenzia delle Entrate for Italy.

### Spain — solidarity tax still in force for 2025 **[P/A]** [Orden HAC/652/2026, 26 Jun 2026, via Tirant, 29 Jun 2026](https://prime.tirant.com/es/actualitat-prime/nueva-regulacion-del-impuesto-a-grandes-fortunas/)

### Portugal — exit tax **[A]** [Bento Castilho Advogados, 17 Dec 2025](https://www.bentocastilho.pt/pt/articles/portugal-exit-tax-individuals-hidden-traps)
- **No general exit tax** on unrealised capital gains for individuals. Portugal taxes gains **only on realisation**; no deemed disposal merely because residence ends. This makes Portugal one of the most exit-friendly regimes in Europe.
- **Four specific exceptions** that act as exit taxes:
  1. **Incorporation of an independent business** into a company under a tax-neutral regime — deferred gains crystallise on loss of Portuguese residence.
  2. **Tax-neutral M&A / share exchanges / restructurings** — deferred gain (original cost vs market value at restructuring date) taxed on exit.
  3. **Cryptoassets — a genuine exit tax.** Loss of Portuguese tax residence is treated as a **disposal**; unrealised crypto gains taxable, standard rate up to **28%** depending on asset/holding period.
  4. **Startup/SME stock options and equity plans** — loss of residence is explicitly a **taxable event** for the deferred/partially exempt gain.
- Assets **not** taxed on exit: shares/securities still held (outside the above), unsold real estate, ordinary portfolios' unrealised gains.

### Ireland — remittance basis still in force; €200k domicile levy **[P]** [Revenue.ie, published 24 Nov 2025](https://www.revenue.ie/en/jobs-and-pensions/tax-residence/domicile-domicile-levy.aspx)
- **Remittance basis still described as current law** on Revenue's page updated **24 November 2025** (i.e. after Budget 2026): an individual who is Irish tax resident but **non-ordinarily resident AND not domiciled** in Ireland pays Irish tax only on (a) Irish-source income and (b) foreign income **to the extent remitted** into Ireland. ⇒ Ireland has **not** followed the UK in abolishing the remittance basis on this evidence.
- **Domicile levy — a hard annual floor**: **€200,000 per year** where the individual is **Irish-domiciled** AND worldwide income **>€1m** AND Irish property **>€5m** AND Irish income tax paid **<€200,000**. Irish income tax paid may be offset; **USC may not**. Valuation date 31 December; pay/file by 31 October of the following year. 'Irish property' = all assets owned in Ireland on the valuation date, with **no deduction for mortgages or loans**.

### Germany — § 6 AStG exit tax, current law + pending ECJ challenge **[A]** [POELLATH, in DER BETRIEB Steuerboard, 12 Aug 2026](https://www.pe-magazin.de/wegzugsbesteuerung-im-kontext-des-vorabentscheidungsverfahrens-gena-macht-der-eugh-die-stundung-zur-pflicht/)
- Scope: **capital-company shareholdings of ≥1%** held by natural persons. Applies to persons **unlimitedly tax liable for ≥7 of the past 12 years**.
- Mechanism: **fictitious disposal at fair market value (gemeiner Wert)** on departure; taxable gain = **FMV at departure − acquisition cost**.
- **Since 1 January 2022** only two options: (a) **immediate payment**, or (b) **instalments over 7 years**, generally **only against security**. The previously available **deferral (Stundung) for EU/EEA cases was abolished from 1 Jan 2022**.
- **Wächtler** (ECJ 26 Feb 2019, C-581/17): exit taxation without a deferral option infringed free-movement rights; a general exclusion of deferral is disproportionate; security can mitigate collection risk. In **obiter dictum** the Court held that **instalments do NOT cure** the inequality — they also create a liquidity disadvantage; deferral until actual realisation is more favourable.
- **⚠️ Pending: ECJ Case C-430/25 "Gena"** (referred by a Polish court). Facts: Italian-US national who moved to Poland on 1 Jan 2023 and plans to leave for Germany (earliest 2028); the Polish exit tax likewise forces immediate or 5-year instalment payment rather than deferral. A finding of EU-law incompatibility would have **indirect spillover to § 6 AStG**. Judgment "ideally" expected within the coming year (≈2027).
- Practitioner view: "In its current form, § 6 AStG is simultaneously an exit barrier and an immigration deterrent." Those planning to leave, and those who left since 2022, should keep assessments open.
- **BMF letter 2 Jun 2025** (see §2) additionally restores **interest-free, unlimited deferral for pre-2022 moves to Switzerland** only.

### New Zealand — Active Investor Plus + 4-year transitional tax exemption **[P]**
- **[P]** [Immigration NZ, Active Investor Plus Visa](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/active-investor-plus-visa/): **Growth category NZD 5,000,000**; **Balanced category NZD 10,000,000**; funds must be transferred to NZ and invested in acceptable investments. Live/work/study **indefinitely**; partner + dependent children **≤24** included. **Permanent residence after 3 years** (Growth) or **5 years** (Balanced) of keeping funds invested. Cost from **NZD 27,470**; approval in principle 80% within 4 months; **6 months** to transfer and invest after approval in principle. **Applications are open.**
- **[P]** [IRD, Temporary tax exemption, last updated 17 Jun 2026](https://www.ird.govt.nz/roles/new-zealand-tax-residents/temporary-tax-exemption): transitional tax residents are **temporarily exempt from tax on most overseas income for ~4 years**. Qualify if: new migrant or returning NZer; became NZ tax resident **on/after 1 April 2006**; **not tax resident at any time in the 10 years before** qualifying. Available **once only**.
  - **Exempt**: overseas interest, dividends, **foreign investment fund (FIF) income**, rent. Foreign superannuation lump sums have their own 4-year rule.
  - **NOT exempt**: income from overseas **employment or personal services**.
  - End date = earlier of 4 years after the end of the month of qualifying (183-day presence or permanent place of abode).
  - Ends early if: claiming Working for Families/Best Start; including exempt income in the IR3 (opt-out is irrevocable); confirming to a foreign jurisdiction and IRD that NZ tax is/will be paid; or telling IRD you no longer want it.
  - ⇒ **years_until_worldwide_taxation = 4** for NZ.

### Japan — exit tax **[A]** [Leo Wealth, reviewed by Daigo Sato](http://leowealth.global/insights/everything-you-need-to-know-about-japan-exit-tax/)
- Introduced **1 July 2015** for Japanese nationals; extended to foreign nationals **1 July 2020**.
- Rate **15.315%** on unrealised gains.
- **Both** conditions must be met: (1) resided in Japan **more than 5 years of the last 10 years**; (2) hold **securities totalling ≥JPY 100,000,000** at departure.
- Covered: **securities** — stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, derivatives, bonds, options. **NOT covered**: cash, bank deposits, **cryptoassets**, real estate.
- **Deferral available up to 10 years** (application required).
- Visa holders **not** subject (Table 1): Diplomat; Highly Skilled Professional, Business Manager; Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services; Entertainer, Skilled Labour, Technical Intern Training; Temporary Visitor; Student. **Table 2** visa holders (spouses/children of Japanese nationals and permanent residents) **are** subject.

### Switzerland — inheritance/gift tax after the 30 Nov 2025 referendum **[A]** [Homburger, Nov/Dec 2025](https://homburger.ch/en/insights/introduction-of-a-federal-inheritance-tax-rejected)
- Referendum **30 November 2025**: voters **rejected by a large majority** the JUSO popular initiative "For a social climate policy — financed fairly through taxation (Initiative for the Future)", which sought a **federal gift and inheritance tax of 50% with a CHF 50 million exemption**.
- ⇒ Inheritance and gift tax remain **cantonal/municipal only**, at cantonal discretion. **No** federal inheritance tax.
- **Schwyz** and **Obwalden** levy **no** inheritance or gift tax; **Lucerne** levies no **gift** tax.
- **Spouses are exempt** in all cantons that levy the tax; **descendants are exempt in virtually all cantons** — exceptions: **Appenzell Innerrhoden, Neuchâtel, Vaud**.
- Inheritance tax is payable by the **heir** under the law of the **canton of residence of the deceased**; gift tax by the **recipient** under the law of the canton of residence of the **donor**.
- ⚠️ Cantonal rates for **non-descendant** beneficiaries vary widely and were not captured at primary-source level — **flagged in Confidence**.
