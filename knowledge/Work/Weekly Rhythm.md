# Weekly Rhythm

The control that keeps [[Platform Delivery Plan]] from drifting. The plan will
not fail because the work is hard. It will fail because a week passes without a
block, and then another.

## The week

- **One 3 to 4 hour focused block.** Non-negotiable, and protected from meetings
  and errands. One block is enough to finish a workstream chunk.
- **Ship one chunk.** A chunk from [[Platform Delivery Plan]], not a ticket. The
  tickets inside a workstream are usually cheaper together than apart.
- **One worklog entry.** What moved, what blocked, what you learned. Short.
- **One ADR per significant decision.** See [[Architecture Decision Records]].
  If no decision was significant enough, that is itself worth noticing.

## Per phase

- One diagram. C4 L1 or L2, or a pipeline sketch. See [[C4 Model]].
- One blog post, written from the work rather than from scratch. The ADRs and
  worklog entries are the raw material.

## Monthly

- **Roadmap review.** Re-read [[Platform Delivery Plan]], re-rank the tracks,
  and record what changed and why. The plan is a hypothesis, not a contract.

## The rule when it slips

If four consecutive weeks do not produce a shipped chunk, the plan is wrong, not
you. Cut scope rather than break the rhythm. A smaller platform that exists beats
a larger one that does not.

## What this is measured against

Four consecutive weeks with a shipped chunk and a worklog entry. Not velocity, not
points, not hours. Consistency at a sustainable size.
